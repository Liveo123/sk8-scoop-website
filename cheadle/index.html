Roads and travel · checked 9 October 2026

Manchester Road: Cheadle Station advance works from 12 October

Allow extra time on foot between the railway bridge and Newland Road. The council says the road will remain open in both directions, but a short pavement section will close with a signed temporary crossing. Work is expected mostly in the evening and overnight for around four weeks.
