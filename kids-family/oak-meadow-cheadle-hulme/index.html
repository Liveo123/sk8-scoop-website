OAK MEADOW — CHEADLE HULME

Oak Meadow is not pretending to be a day-out destination. That is exactly the point. The upgraded play area gives younger children somewhere purposeful to climb, spin and play when you only have an hour.

Good for: younger children, short local outings, quick fresh-air stops.

Checked 25 September 2026 · upgrade reported November 2024

Play equipment upgraded in 2024

Stockport Council reported that the play area received a new mini basket swing, two bee-design springers and a mini rotating dish. The rotating dish has a dipped lip edge intended to make it easier for children to get in and out.

The Council also identifies Oak Meadow as green open space in or close to Cheadle Hulme district centre, which is part of why it works so well as a low-effort local stop.
