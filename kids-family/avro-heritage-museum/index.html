AVRO HERITAGE MUSEUM — WOODFORD

Woodford built aircraft that ended up all over the world, which gives the Avro Heritage Museum a local claim few aviation museums can match. Start with the site story, then add a cockpit visit if guides and weather allow.

The all-white Vulcan is the obvious headline. The stronger reason to visit is the bigger story behind it: design, testing and manufacturing on this former aerodrome.

Good for: aviation fans, families, engineering-curious readers.

Checked 25 September 2026

Practical details

The museum currently lists public opening on Friday, Saturday and Sunday plus bank holidays, 10am–4pm. Tuesday and Thursday are for pre-booked groups, while Monday and Wednesday are closed.

Avro’s current 2026 event listings quote standard museum admission at £9 adults, £7 concessions, £5 for ages 11+, with under-11s free. Current listings also note that cockpit tours can cost a further £2–£3 per person. Check the live page before relying on those prices for a particular date.

If climbing into a cockpit is the whole reason for your trip, check first. Access depends on volunteer guides, aircraft availability and conditions, and is not something to assume will be running continuously.
