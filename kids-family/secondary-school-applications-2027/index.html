Secondary-school applications for September 2027

For SK8 families with a child starting Year 7 next September, the form is now open. The main deadline is 31 October 2026.

The dates worth putting in the calendar

Stockport Council’s published timetable for children starting secondary school in September 2027 is:

  • 1 September 2026: applications opened.
  • 31 October 2026: initial application deadline.
  • 1 November 2026: the application route opens for people who missed the initial closing date.
  • 22 January 2027: deadline to tell the Admissions Team about a change of address.
  • 1 March 2027: national Offer Day.

The important bit now is not racing through the form. It is making sure the schools on your list are the ones you actually want to investigate, and that you understand how each one allocates places.

A useful September checklist

Do the thinking before the deadline becomes urgent

  1. Write down the schools you genuinely want to investigate.
  2. Read each school’s published admission arrangements for 2027 entry.
  3. Note open evenings and visits while they are still useful for the decision.
  4. Check the real morning journey, not just the distance on a map.
  5. Put 31 October somewhere you will actually see it.
  6. Keep any address or supporting evidence you may need together.

Different schools can use different admission criteria. Living nearby, having a sibling at a school or hearing that a school is popular does not by itself guarantee a place. The school’s published arrangements and the council process are what matter.
