October half-term HAF: who qualifies, where the code comes from and what to check

A free place can be a real help over half-term. But there are eligibility rules, booking codes and limited places to navigate.

26–30 October 2026StockportFunded places have eligibility rules

For most schools following Stockport Council's calendar, autumn half-term falls on 26–30 October 2026. Individual school dates may differ. Stockport's Holiday Activities and Food (HAF) programme offers funded places for eligible children, but being on the school holiday does not automatically make every family eligible.

1. Check which free school meal entitlement applies

Funded places are primarily aimed at school-age children eligible for income-related free school meals. Universal infant free meals alone do not automatically confer HAF eligibility. The council also describes a limited referral-based route for some children outside the usual criteria, without guaranteeing places.

2. Look for your booking-code email

If your child qualifies, look for the email from hello@holidayactivities.com containing a code, including your junk folder. A code is not a promise that a specific club still has spaces. If nothing has arrived, use Stockport Council's official guidance rather than guessing a code.

3. Browse clubs and check availability

Use the council's HAF page to find the current booking route and look for activities near your postcode. Confirm location, age range, accessibility, travel, session times and any additional rules with the provider.
