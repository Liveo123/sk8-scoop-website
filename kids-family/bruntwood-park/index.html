BRUNTWOOD PARK — CHEADLE

FREE

One of the easiest places locally to keep different ages happy at once: open parkland for a walk, a large play area, accessible play equipment and a free BMX track for anyone who wants something more energetic. It works as a quick playground trip, a dog walk or a couple of hours outdoors without needing a detailed plan.

Good for: families, younger children, older children, active outings.

Parking is pay-and-display, and pitch-and-putt is separate.

BMX note: use is free, but suitable protective clothing is required.
