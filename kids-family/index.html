Useful caveatFREE ≠ NO EXTRAS

Free means no mandatory admission or participation fee. Parking, tours, food, equipment and other extras can still cost money, so those caveats stay visible rather than hiding in the small print.

Climb into Avro’s aircraft story

Browse all 52 Adventures →
Woodford · aviation + engineering

Avro Heritage Museum

The former Woodford aerodrome gives this more of a local story than a generic aviation museum. The Vulcan is the obvious headline; the better thread is how aircraft were designed, tested and built here.

Current 2026 listings quote £9 adult admission, £7 concessions and £5 for ages 11+, with under-11s free. Cockpit tours can cost extra and depend on volunteer guides and conditions.
