ANSON ENGINE MUSEUM

The museum says engines run every day it is open, with steam engines demonstrated on its monthly steam days. That is the difference between a room of machinery and an outing where you can actually see what the machinery was built to do.

Good for: engineering fans, families and children who would rather watch something move than read another wall label.

Checked 25 September 2026

Current 2026 opening and prices

The museum is currently open 10am–4pm every Friday from 10 April through October 2026, plus listed steam days and special-event dates.

Standard admission is £8 adults, £2.50 for ages 13–16 and free for under-13s. Event days are £10 for adults.

The next currently listed public steam dates after 25 September are the closing weekend on 24–25 October 2026. Dates can change, so use the live events page before travelling.
