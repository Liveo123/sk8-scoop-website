Stand beneath the Lovell Telescope.

One of those places where the headline object is visible from miles away and still looks bigger when you finally stand under it.

JODRELL BANK

Build the visit around the Lovell Telescope, then use the First Light Pavilion and other exhibitions to understand why this patch of Cheshire matters to radio astronomy.

The scale is the useful bit. You do not need to be a telescope enthusiast before arriving for the engineering to make sense once you are standing beside it.

Good for: families, adults, science and engineering fans.

Checked 25 September 2026

Current prices and opening

Jodrell Bank currently lists online admission at £17.10 adult, £14.50 for ages 65+ and students, £11.90 child, and free for under-fours. Companion tickets for carers/support providers are free.

All standard admission tickets are now annual tickets, giving return visits for 12 months. Online booking saves 10% compared with paying on the door.

The site is currently open Tuesday to Sunday, 10am–5pm, with last entry at 3:30pm. Visitor parking is included in admission.

Space Dome shows and some special events cost extra, so keep those separate from the headline admission price.

Jodrell Bank currently warns about local roadworks affecting journeys, so allow extra travel time and check the live site before setting out.
