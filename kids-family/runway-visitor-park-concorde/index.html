RUNWAY VISITOR PARK

The park itself gives you runway views, retired aircraft, a play area and weekend commentary. Concorde is the reason to turn it from a viewing stop into a proper outing: the Classic tour includes the cabin and flight deck.

Good for: families, aviation fans, photographers.

Checked 25 September 2026

Current costs and opening

Admission is free if you arrive by public transport, on foot or by bicycle. Standard parking currently costs £6 up to 2 hours, £12 for 2–4 hours and £15 beyond 4 hours.

The Concorde Classic tour is currently £18 per person and lasts around an hour; free parking is included. Concorde access is guided-tour only and tours do not run every day.

In September the main park is currently open 8am–6pm. Seasonal closing times change through the year, so check the live page before travelling.
