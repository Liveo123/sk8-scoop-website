Stockport station: a new bridge, better access and your chance to comment

Stockport Council and Transport for Greater Manchester invited views on early-stage proposals for Stockport railway station in November 2026. They include new station buildings on both sides of the railway and a bridge linking the town centre and Edgeley, with lifts and stairs.

What might change?

The plans also consider easier walking and cycling routes, more welcoming public spaces and clearer links to buses and the wider Bee Network. These are proposals, not confirmed construction works; the consultation will help shape subsequent design decisions.

Why it matters to readers in SK8

Passengers travelling from Cheadle Hulme and beyond sometimes change at Stockport. Routes across the railway, accessibility and onward connections matter even if Stockport station isn't their starting point.

When is the deadline?

Thursday 19 November 2026, 11.59pm. The council says the online questionnaire normally takes around 5–10 minutes. This deadline takes precedence over conflicting dates in older third-party summaries.
