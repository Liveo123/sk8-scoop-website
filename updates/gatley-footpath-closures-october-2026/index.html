USEFUL UPDATE · GATLEY

Gatley Footpaths 48 and 51: closure order from 26 October

Two familiar shortcuts are covered by a temporary closure order, and the council states that no official diversion will be provided.

Updated 24 October 2026 · Please use the original sources for changing conditions.

Original editorial illustration, not a photograph or navigational map of the actual location.

WHICH PATHS

Exactly what the order covers

Footpath 48 between Yew Tree Grove and Grasmere Road, and Footpath 51 between Motcombe Grove and Footpath 48. The legal order comes into operation on Monday 26 October 2026 to allow housing development works.

Stockport Council says that signs will be placed at or near the paths and that the order may have effect for six months, unless works finish sooner. The reference is JO/19406316/22.

THE IMPORTANT CATCH

No official alternative route

The council’s wording is explicit: “No diversion will be provided.” If these paths are part of your regular walk, check the council’s latest notice and on-site signs before setting off.

We have deliberately not drawn a substitute route or invented accessibility and walking-time claims. Different people and mobility needs require different decisions.

ALSO FROM 26 OCTOBER

Two resurfacing schemes nearby

High Grove Road, Cheadle: proposed works between Gatley Road and around Kingsway School’s entrance, for approximately seven days from 26 October, with restrictions 8am–5pm.

Outwood Road, Heald Green: proposed works between Wilmslow Road and Cross Lane, for approximately 15 days from 26 October, with restrictions 7.30am–5pm.

Both schedules are weather dependent. The council says temporary closures and signed local diversions may apply during working hours. Check again if you are travelling to school, work or an appointment.
