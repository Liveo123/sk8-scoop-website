Blue bins are going back to fortnightly

Useful news, but nothing changes immediately.

Stockport Council has announced that blue-bin collections for paper, card and cardboard will return to a fortnightly service ahead of Christmas 2026.

Not yet

Keep following your current collection calendar.

The council says residents should keep following their current collection calendars until further notice. Updated calendars are due online during September and will be delivered to households from the end of September.

Read the council update

So: useful news, but do not put the blue bin out a week early on our account.
