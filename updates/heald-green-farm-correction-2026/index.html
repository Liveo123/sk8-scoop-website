Correction: Chamber Hall Farm and Daisy Bank Farm

An earlier SK8 Scoop item mixed up two Heald Green farms. Here is the corrected location and attribution.

Two farms, one mix-up

In a previous issue of SK8 Scoop we wrongly associated cattle crossing Simonsway with Daisy Bank Farm. The cattle were linked to Chamber Hall Farm. Daisy Bank Farm was on Finney Lane.

We are sorry for the error, and grateful to the Heald Green Heritage volunteers who identified it. The correction belongs on our website as well as in the newsletter, so the mistake is not left uncorrected in permanent local-history material.

Helen Morgan’s research on Daisy Bank Farm also discusses its connections with the Simpson family and Chamber Hall Farm. If you have original photographs or records about either site, get in touch.
