The A34 works have closed two different bits of “Footpath 96”

Two separate orders, for different works in different places.

The first closure is part of Scheme 12A of the A34 Major Roads Network Improvement Plan. Since 2 August, part of Footpath 96 and all of Footpaths 97A and 97 have been temporarily closed. The council says the order can remain in place for up to six months, although it ends sooner if the work finishes earlier.

The signed diversion runs via Stanley Road, St James’ Way, the path beside St James’ RC High School, Rushside Road, Footpath 104 and the Network Rail bridge over the railway on the A34. The council reference is JO/13933460.

Then there is a second, separate Footpath 96 closure in Heald Green. That one starts from Wilmslow Road and runs for about 350 metres east, and it is there for public safety during housing-development works. It began on 23 July, again for up to six months. Its reference is JO/13712341 and the diversion follows the boundary of the works enclosure.

Don’t mix these up

Check the reference and map before choosing a diversion.
