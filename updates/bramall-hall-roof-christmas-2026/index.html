Bramall Hall’s restored roof and Christmas opening

The roof, bell and bellcote are back. Here are Bramall Hall’s festive opening details, prices and the separate Elf Trail charges.

Look up when you arrive at Bramall Hall. A major roof restoration begun in February 2025 is now complete, with its bell and bellcote restored and original stone roof tiles retained where possible.

Inside, volunteers have decorated Tudor rooms with floral arrangements inspired by Christmas music. Conservation work on the wall paintings continues, so some artworks remain protected.

What to know before visiting

  • Saturday 21 and Sunday 22 November 2026: Hall open 11am–4.30pm, last admission 3.45pm.
  • Normal Hall admission: £7 adults, £5.50 concessions, children under 16 free.
  • Buy ordinary Hall admission on arrival rather than using the Elf Trail booking as a substitute.
  • Separate Elf Trail: £3 per child aged three or over, plus booking fee and normal admission for accompanying adults; reserve a timed place.

These are the 21–22 November details in the council listing, not a guarantee of identical hours throughout December. Check the Hall’s current programme before travelling.
