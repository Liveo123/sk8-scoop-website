Free warm spaces and digital help in SK8 libraries

Free hot drinks, Wi-Fi and computers at Stockport libraries, plus Cheadle Library’s digital support drop-in on 27 November.

Somewhere warm without buying anything

Stockport Council says its libraries offer free warm spaces, hot drinks, computers and Wi-Fi. For SK8 readers, that includes checking the opening hours at Cheadle, Cheadle Hulme, Gatley and Heald Green libraries. You do not need to be in a crisis to use these facilities.

Free digital support in Cheadle

Friday 27 November 2026, 10.30–11.30am, Cheadle Library, 23 Ashfield Road, Cheadle SK8 1BB. A volunteer offers help with devices and everyday digital questions, with beginners particularly welcome. The council lists it as a free support session; check changes with the library before setting off.

Need wider help with bills, benefits or getting online? The council’s warm-spaces page also points to its cost-of-living team and local support directory.
