After twelve years, Cheadle CoderDojo is signing off

Saturday 21 November is the final meeting of Cheadle’s volunteer-led coding club. Since opening twelve years ago, it has helped children and young people explore coding, build projects and discover what they can make with a computer.

Organiser Lisa Brown remembers shy children gradually gaining the confidence to demonstrate their projects. Some former members went on to study computing and other STEM subjects at university.

Final session details

Saturday 21 November, 11am–2pm, at The Upper Room on the first floor of Moseley Hall, 11 Wilmslow Road, Cheadle SK8 1DW. Enter via the Massey Street car park behind Tesco Express. Entry is free but advance booking is essential. Under-13s need an adult with them. Bring a laptop if possible.

Manchester CoderDojo in Ancoats continues, although the Cheadle club is closing. Check the organiser for the latest availability.
