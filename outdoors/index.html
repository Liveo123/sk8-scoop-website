Useful distinctionROUTE ≠ ACCESS

A published walk and the reserve entrance answer different questions. The Ramblers circular starts outside SK8, while the Carrs itself has its own local access point at Brookside Road. Keeping both visible is more useful than forcing them into one neat story.

Climb Marple’s 16 locks, then find the aqueduct

Browse all 52 Adventures →
The climb

16 locks · about 209 ft / 64 m of rise

The flight gains its height over roughly a mile, which is much easier to understand on foot than from a diagram.

The payoff

Aqueduct + railway viaduct

Carry on to Marple Aqueduct, with the neighbouring railway viaduct making the finish feel like an engineering destination rather than just another towpath walk.

No car required

Marple station is close to the towpath

Canal & River Trust says the station is about five minutes from the towpath and around 15 minutes from the bottom lock. The core walk is free.

Reddish · 2–3 hours

Reddish Vale to the viaduct

A 161-hectare green corridor with wetlands, wildlife and the Grade II-listed Tame Viaduct as the obvious target. The current bird-flu warning also applies here.

Open the Reddish Vale guide →
Marple to Macclesfield · 2–4 hours

Cycle a piece of the Middlewood Way

A 16 km traffic-free greenway on a former railway. Cheshire East currently labels that distance inconsistently as 10 or 11 miles, so 16 km is the safer figure. You do not need to ride all of it.

Open the Middlewood Way guide →
Current park warningCHECK BEFORE YOU GO

Stockport Council currently reports bird flu at Etherow and Reddish Vale. Keep to footpaths, keep dogs on leads and do not touch sick or dead birds. This is a time-sensitive warning, so use the live Council parks page for the latest position.

One Stop Away
