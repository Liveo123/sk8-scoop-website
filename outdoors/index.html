A published circular through Gatley Carrs

Ramblers lists this as an easy 2.9-mile / 4.7km circular taking in Gatley Carrs and the Walter Stansby Memorial Gardens. Its published start point is St Luke’s Church, Wythenshawe, not a made-up Gatley start.

Want to start at the Carrs itself?

The Gatley Carrs Conservation Group says the reserve is at the end of Brookside Road. It currently describes the car park as free, but warns that the main gate is locked at dusk.
