Find Chadkirk’s chapel in the woods.

The useful version is not “visit a chapel”. It is a free estate walk where a 16th-century building appears in a much bigger landscape.

Do not make interior access the whole plan. The estate is the dependable part. Treat going inside the chapel as a bonus unless a current opening or event is confirmed.

From 52 Adventures

CHADKIRK COUNTRY ESTATE

Stockport Council describes Chadkirk as a 60-acre farm and country estate in the Goyt valley, with woodland, orchard, a Coronation Meadow and the historic chapel.

The chapel itself is listed Grade II* and includes 16th-century fabric, although parts were rebuilt and altered later. That gives the walk a destination without turning it into a building-only visit.

Good for: families, walkers and local-history readers who want a free half-day rather than a formal attraction.

Checked 25 September 2026

Getting there

The estate address is Vale Road, Romiley. Stockport Council says there is a small car park at the junction of Vale Road and Otterspool Road.

Romiley station is listed as roughly a 10-minute walk away. Paths can be wet after poor weather, so footwear matters more here than a neat itinerary.
