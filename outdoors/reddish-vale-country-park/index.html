REDDISH VALE COUNTRY PARK

Reddish Vale is not one neat ornamental park. Stockport Council describes a 161-hectare green corridor along the River Tame, with walking, cycling and bridleway links to wider trails.

Wildlife includes sand martins and kingfishers along the river banks, with herons, grebes, cormorants, coots, geese and ducks around the ponds and wetlands.

The viaduct gives the outing its landmark. Stockport’s heritage record lists Tame Viaduct as Grade II and dates it to 1875.

Getting there: the main address is Mill Lane, Reddish, SK5 7HE. Council-listed car parks are at Mill Lane, Tiviot Way and behind Woodhall Drive.

Current warning · checked 25 September 2026

Bird flu has been found at Reddish Vale.

Stockport Council’s current parks warning says bird flu has been found in swans and geese here and at Etherow Country Park. Keep to footpaths, keep dogs on leads, do not touch sick or dead birds, and avoid feathers or surfaces with droppings.

That warning can change, so check the live parks page before setting out.
