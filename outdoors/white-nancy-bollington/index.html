WHITE NANCY — BOLLINGTON

White Nancy is Bollington’s best-known landmark, sitting above the town on Kerridge Hill. The Town Council describes it as a roughly 18-foot-high Grade II listed structure built as a summer house by the Gaskell family around 1815.

The point is the climb and the view, not the building interior. The old doorway was filled in long ago, so this is a destination to reach rather than enter.

Good for: walkers, teens and anyone who likes a visible objective.

Checked 25 September 2026

Two route styles

The Kerridge Steps give the more direct, pitched-stone route. Bollington Town Council still describes this as a tough climb in both directions.

A separate woodland path zigzags through the trees before White Nancy appears. It uses flights of wooden steps and can be slippery in wet weather.

Whichever way you go, treat wet conditions seriously and stay on public paths.
