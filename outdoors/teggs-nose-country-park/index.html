Use the quarry as the theme

The official geology trail circles the summit and quarry area, with numbered points tied to the landscape and former stone working. That makes the outing more than a viewpoint.

Good for: families, walkers and geology-curious adults who want a shorter route with something to look for.

Checked 25 September 2026

Current parking and practical details

The car park is open 24 hours, with charges currently applying between 10am and 10pm. Current rates are free for 30 minutes, £1.80 for 1 hour, £3 for 1–3 hours and £4.30 for 3–10 hours. Blue Badge holders park free.

There is no cycling within the country park. The visitor centre by the main car park has toilets, leaflets and information panels.

Seasonal cattle grazing and wet paths can change the feel of the route, so use boots that can get muddy rather than footwear with a more optimistic relationship to Cheshire weather.
