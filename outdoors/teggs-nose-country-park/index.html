TEGG’S NOSE COUNTRY PARK

Tegg’s Nose works at several scales. You can stay close to the quarry and viewpoints for a shorter outing, or use the longer trail towards the reservoirs for a more energetic half day.

Good for: families, walkers and geology-curious adults.

Checked 25 September 2026

Current parking

Cheshire East currently lists the car park as open 24 hours, with charges applying from 10am to 10pm and no overnight parking.

Current charges are free up to 30 minutes, £1.80 for one hour, £3 for 1–3 hours and £4.30 for 3–10 hours. Blue Badge holders park free.

Paths can be wet and uneven, and cattle graze parts of the site seasonally. Wear footwear that suits the weather rather than the café.
