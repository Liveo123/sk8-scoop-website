From 52 Adventures

THE TORRS — NEW MILLS

The Torrs is the “park under the town”: a sandstone gorge cut by the Rivers Goyt and Sett, with industrial remains, bridges, Torrs Hydro and the Millennium Walkway.

The adventure is the contrast. You leave ordinary streets, drop into a steep landscape and suddenly find Victorian industrial fabric and modern engineering stacked beside the rivers.

Good for: walkers, teens, photographers and industrial-history fans.

Checked 25 September 2026

Access needs a little planning

High Peak Borough Council’s current walking page links a dedicated Torrs access leaflet. It describes several approaches with different gradients, steps and surfaces.

Rock Mill Lane includes several flights totalling 51 steps. Station Road is a steep single-track road; a riverside track from the bottom is described as firm, wide and reasonably level. Paths and steps can still be slippery after poor weather.
