From 52 Adventures

SHUTLINGSLOE

The standard Visit Cheshire route starts at Trentabank car park in Macclesfield Forest and makes a 10 km / roughly 6.5-mile circuit over Shutlingsloe, Wildboarclough and Oaken Clough.

At 506 metres, Shutlingsloe is one of Cheshire’s highest summits and the shape explains the local “Matterhorn” nickname.

Good for: regular walkers, fit teens and anyone ready for a proper hill rather than a short viewpoint stroll.

Checked 25 September 2026

What to check before setting off

Trentabank is a pay-and-display car park. The route includes stiles and gates, and there is a steep final climb and descent.

Cheshire East’s current long-term closure list includes rights of way in the Macclesfield and Wildboarclough area. That does not mean every Shutlingsloe route is closed, but it is enough reason to check the live closure notices and on-site signs before relying on an old GPX file.

Carry layers, water and a proper navigation backup. Phone signal should not be your only plan.
