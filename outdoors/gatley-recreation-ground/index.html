Tennis without a booking fee

Stockport Council currently lists one tennis court at Gatley Recreation Ground, SK8 4EN. Its current park-tennis rule says courts are free to use unless a site is specifically listed as requiring booking or payment. Gatley is not listed among the paid or keypad-access courts.

Good for: a quick hit, older children, teens, adults and anyone who already has rackets and balls.

Gatley skatepark

A proper concrete skatepark

The Council’s skate-ramp list currently includes Gatley Park. Its opening announcement describes the Gatley facility as sprayed concrete with a quarter pipe and extended quarter pipe, spine, wallride, bank and grind ledge.

Good for: skateboarders, BMX riders and scooter riders who want a more active local stop rather than another playground.

Worth knowing: the Council’s detailed feature description dates from the 2023 opening announcement, while the current skate-ramp list still includes Gatley. Check the live Council page before making a special trip.

Add Gatley Carrs

The Free & Cheap Guide combines the recreation-ground stop with Gatley Carrs for a simple active-hour idea: tennis, skatepark, then a nature finish. There is no core entry fee for those three activities, though equipment, food and any travel costs are still yours.
