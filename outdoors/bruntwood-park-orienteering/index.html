Try a proper map adventure in Bruntwood Park

There's more to Bruntwood Park than the usual lap of the playground. Its permanent orienteering course lets you use a map to find control points across the park.

At a glance

  • Where: Bruntwood Park, Cheadle, SK8 1HX.
  • Map: £1.50 for a waterproof printed map or PDF, as listed by Greater Manchester Orienteering Activities.
  • Starter options: three yellow courses, from 1.5km to 2.2km.
  • More challenging: two orange courses, 2.5km and 2.7km.
  • Start: near the west end of the central car park.
  • Parking: pay and display.

Before you go

The course was last checked by its maintainers in July 2026. Two control points have moved slightly; the organiser's notes explain the new positions. Wear shoes suitable for parkland and check the updated map rather than relying on memory.

The course is available as a self-directed outdoor activity. Weather, ground conditions, facilities and individual accessibility needs should be checked before travelling.
