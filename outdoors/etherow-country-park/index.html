ETHEROW COUNTRY PARK

Etherow is big enough to feel like a proper outing without a long drive. Stockport Council currently describes 240 acres of greenspace, with more than 200 plant species and more than 100 bird species.

The Council provides a nature-walk guide and links to longer routes through the area. The designated nature reserve itself is permit-only, so stay on public routes and follow site signs.

Getting there: the park is at George Street, Compstall, SK6 5JD. The on-site car park is pay and display. Marple station is around 1.5 km away.

Current warning · checked 25 September 2026

Bird flu has been found at Etherow.

Stockport Council’s current parks warning says bird flu has been found in swans and geese at Etherow Country Park and Reddish Vale. The Council says to keep to footpaths, keep dogs on leads, avoid touching dead or sick birds, and avoid wild-bird feathers or surfaces with droppings.

This warning is time-sensitive. Check the current Council parks page before visiting rather than assuming it still applies unchanged.
