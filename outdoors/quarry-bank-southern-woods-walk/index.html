Quarry Bank Southern Woods: the mapped 1-mile or 1.5-mile walk

A proper published woodland trail to follow, rather than a guess at which turn you should take.

Updated 24 October 2026 · Please use the original sources for changing conditions.

Original editorial illustration, not a photograph or navigational map of the actual location.

THE ROUTE

Start at Quarry Bank’s Mill Yard

The National Trust publishes a Southern Woods walk starting from Quarry Bank Mill Yard, Styal, Wilmslow SK9 4HP. Follow the purple waymarkers on the official route.

Choose the short 1-mile (1.6km) option or the full 1.5-mile (2.4km) route; the Trust gives a duration of approximately 45 minutes. The paths take you through woodland beside the river and mill pond.

ACCESS & PRACTICALITIES

Slopes, dogs and the map

There are slopes on the route, and the full walk is not fully accessible to wheelchairs or pushchairs. Ask the property about current partial access if that matters to you. Dogs are welcome on leads.

Walking the public woodland paths is separate from paid admission to Quarry Bank’s buildings and garden. Check the Trust’s visitor information for current access and parking.

READ THIS BEFORE GOING

Use the Trust’s instructions, not our illustration

The image above is original illustrative artwork. It is not a map, a survey or an instruction to turn at a particular path. Use the National Trust route, which contains the directions, terrain details and access notes.

A shorter outing and a longer trail are both worthwhile options, but the safest choice depends on weather, footwear, mobility and the paths on the day.
