Follow Alderley Edge’s stories into the woods.

Why it earns a place

Alderley Edge has enough geology, mining history and folklore to give the walk a proper theme. The best version is to use one of the National Trust’s themed routes, or book a guided walk when the dates line up.

Good for: walkers, families with older children, geology fans and anyone who prefers a story attached to the path.

Checked 25 September 2026

Current practical details

National Trust parking at Alderley Edge is currently £5 for up to four hours or £8 all day for non-members. Members park free after registering at the machine.

The site has steep and uneven terrain and is mostly unsuitable for wheelchairs and pushchairs. Guided walks are offered on selected dates and can be free but bookable.

Alderley Edge station is around 1½ miles from the property. The National Trust also notes that the nearby B5087 can be busy, so walking from the village still involves road-side sections.
