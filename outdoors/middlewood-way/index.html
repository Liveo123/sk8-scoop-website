MIDDLEWOOD WAY

Cheshire East Council consistently gives the route length as 16 km, although its current pages describe that as both 10 miles and 11 miles. Treat 16 km as the safer figure. The route runs from Macclesfield to Marple along the former Macclesfield, Bollington and Marple Railway.

It is used by walkers, cyclists and horse riders, so the appeal is steady, low-stress movement rather than trying to set a cycling personal best. The Council describes cycling here as easy and scenic and asks riders to approach pedestrians slowly and use a bell.

Well-surfaced sections are listed as suitable for pushchairs and wheelchairs. Toilets are available at Bollington and Nelson Pit car parks. Bollington parking is free; Nelson Pit is pay and display.

Checked 25 September 2026

What to know before setting off

The route is accessible at all times via Bollington and Poynton car parks and the public-rights-of-way network, subject to any temporary closure or diversion notices.

Surfaces are generally good, but wet weather can still mean muddy patches. If you are cycling, remember this is a shared route with pedestrians and horses.
