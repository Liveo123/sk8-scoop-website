EAST AVENUE PARK — HEALD GREEN

A free outdoor exercise stop with fixed gym equipment plus space for basketball, cricket and football. It is useful for teens or adults who are happy to bring a ball and make their own session.

Good for: adults, teens, a free workout, short local outings.

Checked 25 September 2026

Practical details

Stockport Council lists the park at East Avenue, Heald Green, SK8 3BR. It says there are no parking facilities within the park, although surrounding streets offer parking opportunities.

The Council lists Heald Green railway station at about 1.5 km from the park and several bus routes nearby. Check live public-transport information before relying on a particular service.
