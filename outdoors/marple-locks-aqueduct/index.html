From the 52 Adventures Guide

MARPLE LOCKS + AQUEDUCT

Start near Marple and follow the Peak Forest Canal through the flight of 16 locks. Over roughly a mile, the canal climbs about 209 feet / 64 metres. Then continue towards Marple Aqueduct and the neighbouring railway viaduct for the engineering double act.

The Canal & River Trust currently describes the towpath as always open, and Marple station is close enough to make this one work without a car.

Good for: walkers, families with older children, canal and engineering fans.

Cost: the walk itself is free. Travel, parking, food and other extras are separate.

Checked 25 September 2026

Practical detail before setting off

The Canal & River Trust lists town-centre parking and toilets, and says Marple station is around five minutes from the towpath. Its current visitor page also describes the towpath as always open.

Allow for wet or muddy sections after poor weather. Waterside edges are unfenced in places, so this is not a route where small children can simply be allowed to charge ahead while the adults admire the masonry.

Temporary works can still affect individual stretches, so check current Canal & River Trust notices before travelling.
