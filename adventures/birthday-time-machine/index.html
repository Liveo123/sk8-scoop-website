How to run it

You host. They solve the mystery.

01

Before the party

Download the kit, open the start page and read the adult guide. Have a device ready. Print the casebook only if your group would enjoy working on paper.

02

During the game

Read the opening story, let the children decide on answers, and use the host hints if they stall. The browser game checks answers and keeps the story moving.

03

At the finish

Restore the Great Clock, celebrate the detective team and give each child a printed or digital completion certificate.

What you need: a device with a browser and enough screen space for the group, basic pens and paper, and access to a printer only if you choose the optional printed activities. This is a digital download, not a physical party box.

Practical questions

A proper party adventure, without a pile of props.

Will it work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Download and extract the kit before the party. Open the supplied HTML game in a modern browser. The illustrations are embedded; PDFs open as separate files. The protected online portal is currently for family testing, not a promised customer feature.

Do I need to print everything?

No. Printing is optional. The casebook lets children solve the puzzles on paper, but they can also use the screen and ordinary pencils and paper.

Is the game suitable for a large party?

It is designed for ages 8–11 and approximately 4–12 children with an adult hosting. For a large group, have one screen everyone can see and allow time for everyone to contribute. This group-size estimate has not yet been validated in a family playtest.
