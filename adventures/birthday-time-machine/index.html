Practical questions

A proper party adventure, without a pile of props.

Will it work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Download and extract the kit first. The main browser game works offline, and its pictures are embedded. Keep the whole folder when using printable materials.

Do I need to print everything?

No. Printing is optional. The casebook lets children solve the puzzles on paper, but they can also use the screen and ordinary pencils and paper.

Is the game suitable for a large party?

It is designed for ages 8-11 and approximately 4-12 children, with an adult hosting. Try a small group and allow a break for the more demanding final puzzles.
