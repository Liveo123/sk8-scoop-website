Trust and transparency

Editorial and advertising policy.

SK8 Scoop is useful first, local second, cheeky third and accurate always. This page explains how editorial decisions and commercial activity are kept separate.

How editorial choices are made

SK8 Scoop chooses stories, events, businesses and places for their usefulness, relevance to Cheadle, Cheadle Hulme, Gatley, Heald Green and nearby SK8, timeliness, evidence and reader interest. We do not promise to cover everything, and a submission is not a recommendation.

Editorial coverage is independent. A business cannot pay to improve its editorial ranking, secure favourable judgement, prevent legitimate criticism or obtain an independent recommendation.

Advertising and sponsored content

Paid placements are visibly identified as advertising, normally with a clear label such as ADVERTISEMENT or PAID ADVERTISEMENT before the promotional content. We check advertising claims for suitability, but the advertiser remains responsible for supplying accurate, authorised offer details and imagery.

Advertising may purchase agreed, clearly labelled visibility. It cannot purchase favourable editorial coverage. Where paid directory positions or sponsorships are offered, their commercial nature must be obvious and must not be presented as an independent ranking.

Personal or commercial connections

We aim to disclose material personal or commercial relationships where they could reasonably affect how a recommendation or review is understood. A current or previous advertiser may still be covered, praised, questioned or criticised on the same editorial standards as anyone else.

Free community listings and business submissions

Events, openings, classes, useful offers and other local information can be submitted for editorial consideration without payment. Space and checking time are limited, so we may edit, check, delay, decline, correct or remove items that are outdated, unsuitable, unsupported or not useful enough for readers. Payment does not improve their prospects of free editorial inclusion.

Submitters should provide accurate, current details, a usable organiser or official source, and content they have permission to share. See our website and submission terms for how submitted material may be used.

Accuracy and corrections

We check important practical details using organisers, venues, councils or other strong sources where appropriate. Dates, opening times, prices and availability can change after checking. Please confirm time-sensitive details directly before travelling, booking or paying.

If you believe something is wrong, email contact@sk8scoop.com with the item, the suspected error and any useful evidence. We review material correction requests and aim to correct confirmed significant mistakes promptly. Depending on the situation, a correction may appear on the website, in a later newsletter or in a direct message to affected readers. A disagreement about judgement does not by itself establish a factual error.

Listings are not safety guarantees

Appearing in SK8 Scoop does not, by itself, mean a business or event has been independently inspected, regulated, insured or guaranteed by us. We do not call an organisation verified, approved, vetted, trusted or recommended on the basis of payment alone. Any specific checks we claim to have carried out must be accurate.

Affiliate links

If SK8 Scoop may earn a commission from a link, purchase or booking, we explain that relationship clearly close to the relevant content. Commission does not guarantee editorial inclusion or favourable treatment.

Reader privacy and advertiser reports

We do not sell subscriber email addresses to advertisers. Advertisers may receive appropriate aggregate campaign reporting, not a list of individual readers who clicked. See our privacy notice.

Questions and complaints

For editorial questions, corrections, advertising disclosures or an apparent conflict of interest, contact contact@sk8scoop.com. We review the evidence and explain material decisions where appropriate.
