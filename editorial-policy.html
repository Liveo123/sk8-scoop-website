Trust and transparency

Editorial and advertising policy.

SK8 Scoop is useful first, local second, cheeky third and accurate always. This page explains how editorial decisions and commercial activity are kept separate.

Editorial independence

Editorial items are selected for usefulness, local relevance, timeliness, trust, distinctiveness and reader interest. A business cannot pay to improve its editorial ranking, secure favourable judgement or prevent legitimate correction.

Sponsored-content labelling

Paid placements are clearly labelled with wording such as ADVERTISEMENT, SPONSORED or PAID PARTNERSHIP. The label should be visible before the promotional copy, not hidden at the bottom.

Corrections

SK8 Scoop aims to correct material errors promptly. Readers and organisers can report a possible error by emailing contact@sk8scoop.com. Corrections may be made on the website, in a later newsletter or directly with affected readers depending on the significance.

Free community listings

Community events and useful notices may be included without payment. Space is limited. Submission does not guarantee publication, and ordinary announcements may be declined when they do not materially help, surprise or interest SK8 readers.

Business submissions

Businesses and organisations may submit events, openings, offers, classes, jobs and community information. Details must be accurate, current and supported by an official or organiser source where possible. SK8 Scoop may edit, verify, hold, decline or remove a submission.

Privacy and subscriber data

Subscriber information is used to deliver and improve SK8 Scoop. It is not sold to advertisers. Advertisers receive aggregate campaign reporting rather than subscriber email addresses or private personal information.

Affiliate disclosure

When SK8 Scoop may receive a commission from a tracked purchase or booking, the relationship will be disclosed clearly near the relevant link. Affiliate income does not guarantee inclusion or favourable coverage.

Event and offer changes

Dates, prices, availability and terms can change. Readers should check the organiser or business page before travelling, booking or paying.
