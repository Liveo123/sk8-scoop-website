FREE · CHEADLE

Abney Hall Park

Parkland, woodland paths, wetland and open grass. A proper local option for a short walk, families or the dog.

Two free car parks are currently provided.

FREE · CHEADLE

Bruntwood Park

Open parkland, a large play area, accessible play equipment and a free BMX track for a more energetic outing.

Watch the extras: parking is pay-and-display and pitch-and-putt is separate.

FREE · RAINY DAY · STOCKPORT

Hat Works

General museum admission is free, with original machinery, recreated workshop spaces and Stockport's hatting story.

Optional factory-floor guided tours cost extra.
