Questions worth answering

Will my existing adventure disappear if I don't pay?

No. Independent editorial inclusion is not conditional on buying advertising.

Does a featured card put me at number one?

No. We can agree a prominent sponsored position, but payment cannot buy an editorial ranking or recommendation.

Do I have to make the artwork?

No. We can prepare it from approved information and images, or use suitable artwork you supply. You approve the proof before publication.

Can you guarantee bookings?

No. We can report available tracked actions, but enquiries and bookings depend on the offer, audience and other factors.
