The useful question

What exactly are you trying to fill?

“Christmas bookings now open” is information. It is not always a strong reason to choose one venue over another. A useful campaign starts with the particular booking need.

Festive lunches or dinnersPromote the menu, dates and the reason the booking is worth making now.
Party or group bookingsMake group size, dates, package or useful incentive clear.
Christmas DayUse a direct booking action and material price/terms where relevant.
A seasonal eventPromote the actual event and date, not the venue in general.
Current SK8 route

Start with TEST, not the biggest package.

For normal restaurant, pub and venue bookings, the current default is TEST £40: one clearly labelled sponsored newsletter placement built around one reader action. SK8 Scoop prepares the reader-facing advert and you check the facts, offer, links and brand details.

The GROW £90 route is not automatically better for hospitality. It only makes sense when the actual proposition genuinely belongs in the Free & Cheap Guide as well as the newsletter.

Use the 4-step Campaign Finder

Before buying

Three things need to be true.

  1. There is a concrete reason to act. A menu, event, booking window, useful incentive or defined seasonal proposition.
  2. Readers can complete the action. A working booking, enquiry or normal phone/contact route.
  3. There is enough lead time. SK8 Scoop still needs space, creative time and factual approval before publication.

If one of those is missing, the sensible recommendation may be to fix it first or wait. The Campaign Finder is allowed to say that.

What happens after a campaign

Clicks are evidence. Bookings are a different question.

SK8 Scoop records the interaction it can measure, such as tracked clicks. Afterwards, the advertiser is asked what enquiries or bookings they can actually identify. Those two evidence layers are kept separate.

The next recommendation can be repeat, change, stop or wait for the next relevant opportunity. Results are not guaranteed.

Commercial, not editorial

Advertising does not buy a recommendation.

Paid placements are clearly labelled and kept separate from SK8 Scoop's editorial choices. Paying for advertising does not buy editorial coverage, ranking or favourable treatment.
