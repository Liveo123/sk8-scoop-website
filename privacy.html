Privacy

Privacy notice.

SK8 Scoop uses personal information only for clear operational purposes such as sending the newsletter, recording stated email interests, reviewing submissions, responding to enquiries and carrying out proportionate business-to-business advertiser outreach.

Who is responsible for your information?

SK8 Scoop is a local publication operating under JiveLoop. The business operates as a sole trader, not a limited company. The individual sole trader who operates JiveLoop and SK8 Scoop is responsible as data controller for the personal information described in this notice.

JiveLoop / SK8 Scoop business correspondence: 136 Stockport Road, Cheadle Heath, Stockport, Cheshire SK3 0JE, United Kingdom.

For privacy enquiries or requests, email contact@sk8scoop.com.

The data controller is Paul Livesey, a sole trader trading as JiveLoop and operating SK8 Scoop. The address above is our business correspondence address.

Lawful bases and purposes

Our principal uses of personal information and the UK GDPR lawful bases we consider applicable are:

  • Newsletter delivery and optional interests: your email address and any preferences are used to send the requested publication and improve relevance. We rely on consent for marketing where required, with legitimate interests for administering subscriptions and protecting the service. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Messages, events, tips, corrections and feedback: details you provide are used to assess and respond to submissions, verify facts and improve the publication, normally on the basis of legitimate interests in running a useful local service.
  • Advertising enquiries, confirmed bookings and payments: business-contact and booking details are used to assess suitability, prepare agreed services and administer payment, relying on legitimate interests before a contract, contract where applicable, and legal obligations for accounting records.
  • Relevant business-to-business outreach: publicly available business and contact information is considered using legitimate interests where appropriate, with the additional electronic-marketing rules explained below. An objection to direct marketing will be respected.
  • Optional analytics and advertising measurement: we rely on your choice to allow these technologies, where consent is required. Rejecting optional analytics does not prevent access to the website or guides.
  • Service security and basic operational records: we use information needed to protect and maintain the website, respond to abuse and investigate errors, normally under legitimate interests, subject to applicable law.

Providing a newsletter email address or enquiry details is voluntary, but without the information needed for a particular service we may be unable to send the newsletter, reply or arrange a booking. Do not include sensitive personal details in general submissions unless necessary.

Who receives information and international processing

We use service providers for newsletter delivery (MailerLite), website hosting and database processing (Cloudflare), business communications and, for agreed paid services, payment administration (which may include Stripe). If you opt in, Google Analytics and Meta Pixel receive the categories of usage information described below. Providers act in different roles depending on their service. We share relevant details only for purposes needed to provide those services, and we do not sell subscriber lists to advertisers.

Some providers or their subcontractors may process information outside the UK. Where UK rules on restricted international transfers apply, a recognised adequacy arrangement or appropriate contractual safeguards, such as the UK Addendum to standard contractual clauses, may be relevant. The particular destination and transfer mechanism depend on the provider and account arrangements. You may ask contact@sk8scoop.com for details relevant to your information.

How long information is held

Newsletter information is kept while you remain subscribed, subject to appropriate deletion and minimal suppression records needed to respect an unsubscribe or objection. Contact, submission, prospect and campaign information is retained only for as long as needed to deal with the matter, manage the relationship, resolve disputes or meet applicable legal and accounting duties. Relevant retention criteria include the last meaningful contact, ongoing work, the risk of repeat unwanted marketing and statutory obligations.

Some website measurements and feedback have more specific periods described below, including up to 12 months for first-party search insights and Secret Trail beta feedback. The optional tracking choice is stored for up to 90 days. Browser-only saved items remain on the device until removed or browser storage is cleared. To ask about retention or deletion of specific information, contact us using the address above.

Newsletter subscriptions

MailerLite processes newsletter subscriptions and email delivery. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. The initial signup asks for an email address only.

Optional interests

Readers may voluntarily state interests such as family activities, events, food, offers or practical updates. These preferences are used to improve relevance and can be changed later.

Reader, business, event and contact submissions

Information sent through SK8 Scoop forms is used to review the submission, administer the relevant competition or feature, verify information where needed and respond to the sender. This can include a name, email address, message, link or the item the submission relates to.

Submitting information does not automatically publish the sender's contact details.

Secret Trail beta feedback

The Cheadle Secret Trail feedback form asks about trail completion, age range, approximate time, difficult parts, real-world problems and whether a child would do another trail. It does not ask for a name, email address or child’s name.

Feedback responses and campaign tags may be stored in SK8 Scoop’s Cloudflare D1 database for up to 12 months so the beta can be evaluated and improved. Free-text boxes are intended for trail feedback, not contact details; obvious email addresses and phone numbers are redacted before storage. Optional Google Analytics events for the feedback page or successful submission are recorded only if analytics consent has been given.

Business prospecting and advertiser outreach

SK8 Scoop may use publicly available business information to identify organisations that appear relevant to the SK8 audience and to make proportionate business-to-business contact about advertising. Public availability does not by itself mean that every contact route is suitable, so we check the business, legal entity, recipient role, source and any previous objection before outreach.

Where a named business contact or other personally identifying business information is used, UK data protection law applies. Where appropriate, SK8 Scoop may rely on legitimate interests for targeted business-to-business marketing after considering necessity, proportionality and the person's reasonable expectations. We use only business-relevant information needed for that purpose and record the source of the information.

We do not treat sole traders and certain partnerships in the same way as limited companies for unsolicited electronic marketing. Where consent or another applicable basis is required, we do not send cold electronic marketing without it. Social-media direct messages are treated as electronic messages for this purpose.

Every marketing contact identifies SK8 Scoop and gives a simple way to object or opt out. If someone objects to direct marketing, we stop and retain only enough information on a suppression record to respect that preference in future. Questions or objections can be sent to contact@sk8scoop.com.

My SK8 saved items

The experimental My SK8 feature stores your saved event IDs, reminder choices and a small copy of the event details in your own browser using first-party local storage. You do not need an account, and this saved list is not sent to SK8 Scoop just to make the feature work.

If you share a shortlist, the selected event IDs are included in the share link so the recipient can open the same public event list. The link does not include your name, email address or a My SK8 account because this version has no account system.

If you have allowed optional analytics, SK8 Scoop may measure actions such as viewing the save controls, saving or removing an item, setting a reminder, opening directions, adding an event to a calendar, viewing the weekend plan or sharing a shortlist. These measurements are used to decide whether the feature is useful. Rejecting optional analytics does not stop My SK8 from working.

You can remove individual saved items in My SK8. Clearing this site’s browser storage will also remove the saved list and reminder choices from that browser.

Subscriber guide access

Current SK8 Scoop subscriber guides can be unlocked by entering an email address. If the address is already subscribed, the website uses that submission to recognise the current browser. If it is not already subscribed, the same action signs the address up to the free Friday SK8 Scoop email.

After a successful signup or recognition, SK8 Scoop sets a first-party browser access token so eligible subscriber guides and future subscriber extras can be opened without repeatedly entering the email address. The token does not contain the email address and is intended to remain until browser data is cleared, although browsers may apply their own storage limits.

This is deliberately a lightweight subscriber gate rather than a password account system. Treasure Hunts and paid or referral-only products such as future Pub Trails are not included in subscriber guide access.

Analytics and tracking

Google Analytics is optional. If you allow it, Google Analytics measures page visits, referral sources and useful actions such as form submissions and campaign-link clicks. Advertising signals and Google ad personalisation are disabled in this site’s configuration.

Meta Pixel is optional. If you allow it, Meta may receive page visits and selected website actions to measure Facebook advertising and build advertising audiences.

Neither optional service loads before you make a choice. The site remembers that choice for up to 90 days. Use the Privacy choices button at the bottom of any page to change or withdraw it.

QR poster reporting records the poster or venue associated with a visit in aggregate. It does not store the visitor’s email address in the QR analytics database.

First-party search insight: when you submit a search on SK8 Scoop, the search phrase, number of results shown and selected search filters may be stored for up to 12 months so we can see what local information readers are looking for and where the site has gaps. The search log does not store your email address, IP address or a device identifier. Obvious email addresses, phone numbers and web links typed into the search box are redacted before storage. Search phrases are used as editorial evidence, not sold or shared with advertisers.

Advertising data

Advertisers may receive aggregate reporting such as campaign clicks. Subscriber email addresses and private personal data are not sold or shared with advertisers for their own marketing.

Your privacy rights and complaints

Depending on the circumstances and the legal basis for processing, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or transfer personal information, and to object to particular uses. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it. You can object to the use of your personal information for direct marketing at any time.

To make a privacy request or complaint, email contact@sk8scoop.com and explain what you want us to look into. We may need to confirm your identity before disclosing or changing personal information. We will assess the request against the applicable legal requirements and explain the result.

If you remain concerned about how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner\u2019s Office (ICO).
