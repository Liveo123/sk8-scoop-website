Privacy

Privacy notice.

SK8 Scoop uses personal information only for clear operational purposes such as sending the newsletter, recording stated email interests, reviewing submissions and responding to enquiries.

Newsletter subscriptions

MailerLite processes newsletter subscriptions and email delivery. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. The initial signup asks for an email address only.

Optional interests

Readers may voluntarily state interests such as family activities, events, food, offers or practical updates. These preferences are used to improve relevance and can be changed later.

Business and event submissions

Contact details and submitted information are used to verify the item, respond to the sender and manage possible publication or advertising activity.

Analytics and tracking

When configured, analytics may record page visits, referral sources, form interactions and campaign-link clicks. QR poster tracking may record the poster or venue associated with a visit. Private keys and confidential account information are not exposed in public website files.

Advertising data

Advertisers may receive aggregate reporting such as campaign clicks. Subscriber email addresses and private personal data are not sold or shared with advertisers for their own marketing.

Your requests

You may ask to access, correct or delete information SK8 Scoop holds about you, subject to applicable requirements. Email contact@sk8scoop.com.
