52 Adventures · Stockport Market Place

Eat your way around Foodie Friday.

Stockport’s monthly street-food evening is a simple local adventure: turn up hungry, order different things and compare notes.

Check the current listingOpen 52 Adventures
Usual patternLAST FRIof the month6pm–9pm

What the current Council listing says

Stockport Council currently lists Foodie Friday at Market Place on the last Friday of each month from 6pm to 9pm, with street food, drink and entertainment.

The Council’s event listing shows free entry. Food and drink are, naturally, the bit that costs money.

Make it an adventure

Nobody orders the same thing.

Foodie Friday earns its place in 52 Adventures because it changes how you use the town centre after work. Split up, pick different traders, share bites and leave with one thing you would deliberately come back for.

Allow: around 1½–2½ hours, longer if you carry on into the Old Town afterwards.

Before you go

Check the actual date.

The regular pattern is the last Friday of the month, but individual dates can move around holidays. The live Council events page is the safer source for the specific month you plan to visit.

Market Place is busier than on an ordinary evening, so public transport can be easier than assuming nearby parking will be quiet.
