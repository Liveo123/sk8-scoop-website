The Chiverton Tap: a pub with an old shop name under your feet

The first thing to look at isn't the beer. It's the little piece of Cheadle Hulme history still visible in the doorstep.

8 Mellor Road, SK8 5AUIndependent micropubOpened May 2015

If you walk into The Chiverton Tap, look down. The entrance mosaic still bears the name Arthur Chiverton, whose drapery and millinery business occupied the premises for around 70 years. The pub took its name from that earlier shop, rather than wiping the history away.

Why it's worth knowing: a visible fragment of the old High Street survives in a working local business. You don't need a tour or a historical reconstruction to see what the building used to be.

From fabric shop to micropub

The former draper's shop became a micropub in May 2015. Stockport and South Manchester CAMRA identifies it as Stockport's first micropub and records later additions to the upstairs and outdoor seating. The entrance mosaic is still there, according to CAMRA's venue history.

What to expect today

The pub describes a changing selection of independent beers, wines and spirits. CAMRA mentions a three-thirds-of-a-pint tasting option, sometimes called “beer tapas”, and locally supplied pies. Stock and prices change, so treat these as a description rather than a guaranteed offer.

The pub is close to Cheadle Hulme station on Mellor Road. Check its own website for current opening times and today's drinks before setting out.
