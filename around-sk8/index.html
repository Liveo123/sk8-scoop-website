The Heald Green spy and the future Dambuster

Heald Green · local history case file

One local thread places First World War spy Marthe McKenna in a bungalow near Heald Green Hotel. Another locally recorded account says Guy Gibson and around 40 RAF men used the hotel’s upstairs Committee Room during the war. The intriguing bit is how close the two stories sit without pretending the current sources prove a direct connection.
