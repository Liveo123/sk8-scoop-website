Three proposed housing sites in Heald Green

Heald Green · Local Plan · Consultation deadline: 8 November 2026

Stockport's published Local Plan names three proposed Heald Green housing allocations. An allocation is not planning permission. The Regulation 19 consultation focuses on legal compliance and soundness, rather than a general vote on development.
