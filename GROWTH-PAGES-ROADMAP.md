# Growth page publishing roadmap

These pages are prepared as future editorial products but are deliberately not published as weak placeholders.

| Proposed page | Publish only when it has | Suggested update cycle |
|---|---|---|
| New to SK8 | Verified starter information across all core areas, transport, services and useful local links | Quarterly |
| Free and cheap things to do | At least 15 current verified entries with clear prices and dates | Fortnightly |
| Kids eat free | Verified terms, ages, days, end dates and direct business sources | Weekly |
| SK8 Boredom Button | At least 25 verified options tagged by weather, age, cost and area | Weekly |
| Parks and playgrounds | Useful first-hand or authoritative detail for at least 12 locations | Quarterly |
| Dog-friendly cafés | Direct venue confirmation of dog policy for at least 10 places | Monthly |
| Holiday clubs | Current dates, ages, prices, booking links and availability caveats | Each school holiday |
| Public noticeboards | Exact locations, access rules and evidence for each board | Quarterly |
| Sunday breakfast | Verified Sunday hours, prices and practical notes for at least 10 places | Monthly |

Each published page should include source URLs, a checked date, a clear SK8 angle and a reminder to verify time-sensitive details before travelling or paying.
