05 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

The questions worth asking.

We would rather say a campaign isn't ready than take a fee for the wrong one.

Why pay more than the £40 newsletter advert?

You can still buy the existing £40 TEST if one newsletter advert is all you need. The proposed £199 pack adds a finished feed image and caption, a Story image and a shareable PDF flyer, all adapted from the same approved offer, plus an advert click report. The newsletter placement is included, not charged twice.

Will you guarantee bookings or enquiries?

No. We can agree the goal and track some actions, but the result depends on the offer, demand, distribution and the business's own response. We'll distinguish measurable clicks from enquiries or bookings confirmed by the business.

Does SK8 build or edit my booking page?

No. You need an existing, working booking or quotation destination. We check that the link works and use it consistently in the advert and supplied material. We do not develop, host or edit your website as part of this pack.

Who publishes the social posts and flyer?

You do. We deliver the finished PNG images, PDF flyer and caption for your business to use. SK8 Scoop's publication obligation is one clearly labelled newsletter advert in the edition agreed in writing. Paid Facebook or Instagram campaigns and SK8 social posts are not included.

When do I get everything?

Proposed timetable: first artwork proof within five working days after receiving a complete brief and approved imagery, then publication in the newsletter edition agreed before payment. The tracked-click summary follows seven days after sending. We confirm timing and one revision round in writing before taking a booking.

Can paying for this affect editorial coverage?

No. SK8 Scoop's editorial choices, rankings and recommendations stay independent. Any paid SK8 content is clearly labelled as advertising or sponsorship.
