05 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

The questions worth asking.

We would rather say a campaign isn't ready than take a fee for the wrong one.

Is this just an expensive newsletter advert?

No. The proposed service is campaign planning, practical creative, improvement recommendations and measurement. A newsletter or guide placement is optional, separately priced and subject to suitability.

Will you guarantee bookings or enquiries?

No. We can agree the goal and track some actions, but the result depends on the offer, demand, distribution and the business's own response. We'll distinguish measurable clicks from enquiries or bookings confirmed by the business.

Do I need an existing website?

You need a working, suitable place for customers to enquire or book, such as your own website or an established booking service. Building a whole new website isn't part of this fixed-scope pilot.

Will you run advertising on every channel?

No. We agree practical, available channels before starting. Paid media budgets, third-party placements and ongoing account management are not included in the proposed fee.

Can paying for this affect editorial coverage?

No. SK8 Scoop's editorial choices, rankings and recommendations stay independent. Any paid SK8 content is clearly labelled as advertising or sponsorship.
