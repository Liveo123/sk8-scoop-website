# NUE Facebook Acquisition v1

## Purpose
Test SK8 Scoop acquisition using useful content first, with subscription offered after value has been demonstrated.

## Campaign A: Free & Cheap Guide

### Landing page
`/free-cheap/`

### Promise
Free and low-cost things to do around SK8, organised by immediate reader need.

### Ad creative message
- FREE & CHEAP THINGS TO DO AROUND SK8
- Free
- Under £5
- Under £10
- SK8 Scoop

### Primary text
Looking for something to do without spending a fortune?

We’ve put together the SK8 Scoop Free & Cheap Guide, packed with local ideas that are free, under £5 or under £10.

Use it when you need an afternoon out, something with the kids, somewhere indoors, or simply an idea you hadn’t thought of.

### Headline
Free & cheap things to do around SK8

### Description
Local ideas from SK8 Scoop

### CTA
Learn More

### Tracking URL
`https://www.sk8scoop.com/free-cheap/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=free_cheap_guide_sep_2026&utm_content=guide_ad_v1`

Do not change the existing first-touch attribution model. The shared site script should preserve first meaningful acquisition for 90 days and populate MailerLite attribution on later signup.

### Funnel events to measure
1. Paid landing-page visit
2. Price/situation route interaction
3. Full-guide open
4. Next-experience click
5. Join-page visit
6. Genuine subscriber conversion

### Initial budget
Start small. Suggested test: £5/day for 5-7 days after landing-page, full-guide route, analytics and signup protection are confirmed.

### Launch gate
Do not spend until:
- full v22.2 guide is connected from `/free-cheap/`
- paid-social UTM capture has been tested
- signup anti-spam protection is working or the paid funnel does not expose an unprotected new signup form
- mobile QA passes
- guide links are checked

## Campaign B: NUE website experience

### Principle
Do not advertise the homepage or newsletter as the product. Advertise one immediately useful local experience.

### Candidate promise
`What’s worth knowing around SK8 right now?`

### Acquisition page structure
1. One important/useful current item
2. One worthwhile thing to do
3. One distinctive SK8 story
4. Free & Cheap Guide as a natural second experience
5. Explore SK8 routes: history, walks/cycling, planning
6. Contextual Friday newsletter invitation after value

### Tracking URL pattern
`https://www.sk8scoop.com/around-sk8/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=nue_experience_sep_2026&utm_content=<creative_id>`

### Core metric
NX rate = percentage of acquired users who move from their first useful experience into a second SK8 experience.

Secondary metrics:
- landing-page engagement
- second-experience rate
- guide/open-content interaction
- join-page visit rate
- subscriber conversion
- cost per genuine subscriber

## Testing rule
Change one major variable at a time where practical. Do not simultaneously change audience, creative, message and landing experience and then pretend the result explains why performance moved.

## Low-workload rule
Campaign pages should be fed by existing approved SK8 content and records. No separate weekly research process should exist purely to keep paid acquisition pages alive.
