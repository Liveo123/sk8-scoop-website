Advertising booking terms.

A simple explanation of what an SK8 Scoop advert includes, how bookings work and what happens if something changes.

Who you are booking with

SK8 Scoop is published by Paul Livesey, a sole trader trading as JiveLoop. SK8 Scoop is a local publication, not a limited company. Business correspondence: 136 Stockport Road, Cheadle Heath, Stockport, Cheshire SK3 0JE. Email: contact@sk8scoop.com.

Enquiry, quotation and agreement

An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. Before asking you to pay, we agree in writing what you are buying: the advert format and surface, selected Guide or newsletter issue, start and end dates (where relevant), section or position if specifically included, destination link, price and any applicable tax, and important special conditions. Availability and suitability are checked before confirmation. Your booking is accepted when we explicitly confirm it and the agreed payment conditions are met. An advertised price alone does not reserve a date or location.

What paid placements include

Products may include a clearly labelled advert in one weekly newsletter, a sponsored card in an agreed Guide, a combined newsletter and Guide campaign, or a more prominent Guide sponsorship. Your written confirmation defines the actual placement. Where a year-round Guide card is offered, its normal display period is 30 days from the agreed start date. Seasonal placements run for the specific agreed promotional window. Unless your agreement specifically says otherwise, packages are one-off purchases and do not renew automatically.

A sponsored position does not imply category exclusivity, a business endorsement or preferential treatment in independent editorial coverage. We will only agree to limited paid-inventory exclusivity if its exact category, surface and dates are explicitly confirmed in writing. See the editorial policy.

Advert wording, artwork and approval

You tell us the facts, offer restrictions and what you want readers to do. We can prepare the advert for your review or use suitable copy, imagery or logo that you supply. By supplying material you confirm that you have permission for SK8 Scoop to reproduce and adapt it as agreed for your campaign. You remain responsible for the truthfulness of your business claims and special-offer conditions. We remain responsible for preparing and publishing the agreed placement with appropriate care.

You receive the advert for approval before publication. We correct our own material errors without an undisclosed fee. Requests that materially change a previously agreed brief or artwork may require a revised date, scope or separately agreed price. We may decline or remove material that is unlawful, materially misleading, inappropriate for our readership or incompatible with the publication's standards, subject to fair treatment of any amount already paid.

Payment, cancellation and missed delivery

We state the full price, any applicable tax, and payment arrangements before confirming a booking. Payment is normally due before publication unless we agree otherwise in writing. Any material cancellation conditions will be explained before you commit. We do not impose an undisclosed cancellation charge or assume all payments are automatically non-refundable.

If SK8 Scoop cannot supply an agreed paid placement, we will offer an agreed suitable alternative date or an appropriate refund for the undelivered part. If something material is published incorrectly, tell us promptly so we can investigate and offer a fair correction, replacement or proportionate remedy. A cancellation requested by the advertiser is considered against its timing and any clearly agreed, reasonable costs already incurred. Existing agreements are not silently changed by later updates to this page.

Reporting and results

Where relevant tracking is available, we provide a short summary of measured advert-link activity after the agreed campaign period. Newsletter and Guide clicks may be reported separately when they are distinguishable. Reported clicks are not verified leads, sales or bookings; consent choices, privacy protections and tracking limits may affect measurement. We do not promise a minimum number of clicks, enquiries, customers or sales, or imply that the measured number is a complete count of human interest.

Complaints and legal rights

If anything seems wrong, email contact@sk8scoop.com with the campaign details and the issue. We will consider the evidence and work towards a fair practical resolution. Nothing in these terms removes any right or liability that cannot lawfully be restricted. These terms should be read with our general website terms and privacy notice. Any individually agreed written booking terms take precedence over a later general website update.
