Yuletide at Tatton Park: folklore, glowing gardens and a little fire juggling

It's not simply a circuit of Christmas lights. Tatton Park's Yuletide combines an illuminated garden trail with live music, stories, performers and rather unusual winter characters.

SK8 Scoop guide · Checked against organiser information on 10 November 2026 · Tatton Park, Knutsford

At a glance
Selected dates from 14 November to 23 December 2026. Timed entry is required. For 14, 21 and 22 November, tickets are £19.50 for adults, £11.50 for children, and free for ages two and under. Parking is free from 2pm, with an optional £1.50 environmental donation.

What actually happens?

You choose a timed entry, enter Tatton Park Gardens and explore at your own pace. There are two connected parts: a Yuletide Gathering, with live music, storytelling, food and performances, and the Glittering Trail, leading through the trees past glowing installations, illuminated puppets and characters inspired by winter folklore.

The organiser also describes acrobats, orchard wassailers, musicians and unexpected performances along the route. Rather than racing through one sequence of lights, you can stop and enjoy whatever is happening around you. It is a theatrical outdoor event, so the experience may differ from evening to evening.

How long does it take?

Allow around 90 minutes, although the organisers say you can stay longer. There is a small discrepancy in the published distance: the tickets page says 1km, while the access and FAQ pages say approximately 1.4km. We'd plan for the longer distance and treat the route as an outdoor winter walk.

The path is described as step-free, with gravel and dirt paths, grass and hardstanding, plus some gentle gradients. Wear sturdy shoes, wrap up warm and check the organiser's access guidance if you're planning a visit with a wheelchair or buggy.

Tickets, dates and parking

  • Off-peak dates: 14, 21 and 22 November. Adult £19.50; child £11.50; age two and under free.
  • Peak dates: 28 November and the listed December dates. Adult £23; child £14; age two and under free.
  • Entry: Choose a timed slot when booking. The booking page lists slots between 3pm and 7.30pm; check your ticket for the exact arrival time.
  • Parking: Free from 2pm on event days. The organisers request an optional £1.50 environmental donation.
  • Getting there: Use the Rostherne entrance to Tatton Park; the organiser gives WA16 6SG for sat nav.

Worth it from SK8?

It is a journey rather than a quick neighbourhood outing, and a family ticket can add up. But if you want something more theatrical than a conventional light trail, with performers and winter folklore woven into the evening, this is an unusual option. Check remaining time slots before setting off, particularly for the opening weekend.
