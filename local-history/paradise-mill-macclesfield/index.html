Why Paradise Mill is different

Paradise Mill keeps its Jacquard handlooms in their original factory setting. The Silk Museum says it holds the largest collection of silk Jacquard handlooms in their original setting, which is why the guided version earns the extra cost if the times work for you.

Good for: adults, older children, engineering-curious visitors and anyone interested in how Macclesfield’s silk industry actually worked.

Checked 25 September 2026

Current prices and access

The Silk Museum itself is free. Paradise Mill self-led admission is currently £7.50 adult, £6.50 concession and £4.50 child. Guided tours are £14.50 adult, £12.50 concession and £4.50 child.

Paradise Mill is a listed industrial building with uneven floors and tighter spaces. It can be reached by a refurbished lift suitable for wheelchairs or by three flights of stairs totalling 49 steps.
