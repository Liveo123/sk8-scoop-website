52 Adventures · Macclesfield

Hear Paradise Mill’s looms.

The Silk Museum gives you the town story for free. Paradise Mill gives you the machinery in the place it actually worked.

Allow1½–2 HRSSilk Museum free. Paradise Mill currently from £7.50 adult self-led or £14.50 guided.
Why this version works

Use the museum for context, then go upstairs for the machines.

Rainy-day choice
MuseumFree entry

The Silk Museum covers Macclesfield’s silk, design and manufacturing history without a mandatory admission charge.

MillJacquard handlooms in situ

Paradise Mill keeps a large collection of handlooms in their original industrial setting, which is the bit that makes the visit more than display cases.

AccessLift + 49-step stair route

The listed mill has uneven floors and tight spaces. A refurbished lift provides wheelchair access, with the stair route totalling 49 steps.

From 52 Adventures

Current ticket options

The Silk Museum is currently free to enter, with donations welcomed. Paradise Mill has two main options.

Self-led Paradise Mill admission: currently £7.50 adult, £6.50 concession and £4.50 child, with afternoon timeslots.

Guided Paradise Mill tour: currently £14.50 adult, £12.50 concession and £4.50 child. Guided tours are listed at 11am and 12:15pm.

Checked 25 September 2026

Opening pattern

The museum and mill are currently open Wednesday to Saturday, 10am–4pm. Paradise Mill is time-slotted, so booking ahead is sensible if the mill is the main reason for your trip.

Good for: adults, older children, industrial-history readers and anyone interested in the connection between textile machinery and early programmable systems.
