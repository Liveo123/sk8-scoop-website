From the Free & Cheap Guide

A free urban treasure hunt

Treat the Underbanks as a place to nose around rather than a route to complete: steep little streets, old shopfronts, carved stonework, alleyways, painted walls and listed buildings packed into a compact part of town.

Good for: adults, couples, photography, an hour or two, pairing with the Market or museums.

Checked 25 September 2026

What the Council currently highlights

Stockport Council describes the Underbanks as the area around Great Underbank, Little Underbank, Rostron Brow and Lower Hillgate. It is part of the historic Old Town and a designated conservation area.

The Council’s street-art trail includes work tied to Jimi Hendrix’s 1967 Stockport gig, local wildlife, independent businesses and the Tin Brook running beneath the area.

Stockport bus station is roughly a 7–10 minute walk away, and the railway station is also within walking distance.
