Current admission

The National Trust currently lists standard admission without Gift Aid at £16 adult and £8 child aged 5–17. Under-fives and National Trust members are free.

Good for: families, architecture fans and people who usually find stately homes a bit too polished.

Checked 25 September 2026

Historic building, real access limits

The hall has uneven floors, cobbles and steep spiral staircases. The current accessibility information says powered mobility vehicles and motorised wheelchairs up to 0.5 metres wide can enter the lower level, with manual wheelchairs available to borrow.

Opening patterns vary seasonally, so use the live National Trust page for your date rather than relying on a permanent website article for exact hours.
