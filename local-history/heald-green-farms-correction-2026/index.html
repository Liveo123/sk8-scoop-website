LOCAL HISTORY · CORRECTION · 14 NOVEMBER 2026

The cows on Simonsway came from Chamber Hall Farm

Correction and apology: In our Friday 13 November newsletter, we mistakenly attributed the cattle crossing Simonsway to Daisy Bank Farm. Heald Green Heritage has confirmed the farm was Chamber Hall Farm. Daisy Bank Farm is a separate property on Finney Lane. We're sorry for the confusion and grateful to Kathy at Heald Green Heritage for putting us right.

Local memories describe cattle being taken across Simonsway twice a day. Heald Green Heritage's research includes recollections from Sarah Bayley of drivers waiting for the herd, with some motorists reportedly losing wing mirrors when trying to squeeze past.

Daisy Bank Farm has a different story

Heald Green Heritage published research on Daisy Bank Farm on 4 November 2026, exploring early settlement around the Styal Road and Finney Lane area and the history of the farmhouse. Its researchers have also raised concerns about the site's future amid housing-development proposals.

Those proposals need careful distinction: a proposed housing allocation is not planning permission or confirmation that demolition will happen. A local ratepayers' summary of the September 2026 draft Local Plan describes a Daisy Bank allocation while also saying the draft policy requires retention of Daisy Bank Farm. We are checking the formal planning documents before making any stronger claim about what may happen to the building.

Sources: Heald Green Heritage's Daisy Bank Farm research; Heald Green Ratepayers' housing-site analysis; direct correspondence with Heald Green Heritage, 13 November 2026.

Why this matters

Confusing the farms risks losing two different pieces of local history. The Simonsway cattle memories belong to Chamber Hall Farm. The question of Daisy Bank Farm's heritage and proposed development deserves its own evidence-based investigation, not a recycled cow anecdote.
