Why go

Staircase House works because you move through the history rather than look at a collection that could sit anywhere. The rooms show how the house changed over centuries, and the historic fabric makes a useful counterpoint to grander heritage houses.

Turn it into a small Old Town day: Staircase House, Market Place and the Underbanks, then food or coffee nearby.

Checked 25 September 2026

Current prices and opening

Stockport Council currently lists adult admission at £6, concessions at £4.50, and under-16s free with a paying adult. Leisure Key holders are free.
