LOCAL HISTORY · HEALD GREEN

Reda’s, Pat Phoenix and the boxing kangaroo of Heald Green

A television shop, a Coronation Street star and one of the stranger local memories to emerge from Finney Lane.

Updated 24 October 2026 · Please use the original sources for changing conditions.

Original editorial illustration, not a photograph or navigational map of the actual location.

THE PLACE

From bank to televisions

The building at 225–229 Finney Lane was still recorded as a bank in 1940. By 1947 it was Reda’s, meaning Radio and Electrical Domestic Appliances. Old advertisements help show the business and the changing way local families acquired televisions.

This account is drawn from the work of Heald Green Heritage, which brings together documents and residents’ memories. We have not copied the site’s privately credited historic photographs.

THE GRAND REOPENING

Pat Phoenix and the kangaroo

Local residents remember actor Pat Phoenix, known from Coronation Street, attending a grand reopening in 1963. Multiple memories also mention a kangaroo wearing boxing gloves. One former child resident recalls Saturday 18 May 1963 as the date, recorded in her diary. Another remembers a naming competition and the name “Redaroo”.

Those are remembered details, not independently verified records of the programme. In particular, a boxing costume is not evidence that the animal fought a person. The shop’s history is strange enough without inventing a contest.

WHY IT MATTERS

Ordinary shopfronts, unexpectedly vivid stories

The electrical shop, the area’s bank history and surviving advertisements are part of the commercial life of Heald Green. The stories passed down by residents add colour, but keeping evidence and recollections separate is essential.
