Local history · Heald Green

A four-mile history walk hiding in plain sight

Heald Green Heritage’s North History Walk links 22 pieces of local history into one roughly four-mile, two-hour route.

Checked18 SEP 2026Route facts checked against Heald Green Heritage’s current walks material used for Issue 13.

Why it works

It joins the area’s history into one route

The interesting part is the mix: Heald Green station and the old telephone exchange, Manchester Airport, former farms, local pubs, Cheadle Royal, war memorials, the theatre, churches, St Ann’s Hospice and the library all become part of one route rather than separate bits of local trivia.

Before walking it

Use the Heritage route for navigation

Heald Green Heritage’s walks page was last updated in May 2026, while the underlying North Walk audio was first published in 2022. Stockport Council currently has a signed diversion on part of Footpath 96 from Wilmslow Road. The Heritage walk does not identify numbered public rights of way clearly enough for SK8 Scoop to confirm whether that exact segment is part of the route.

Check the Heritage route and follow current signed diversions on the ground. SK8 Scoop has not physically audited the full route for accessibility.

Paul’s note

The places are familiar. The stories usually are not.

I knew plenty of the places on this route, but not the stories behind them. I think that’s what makes local history interesting: you can walk past something hundreds of times without knowing what was there before.
