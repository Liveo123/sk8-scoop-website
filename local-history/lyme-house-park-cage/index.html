The estate only really makes sense once you leave the house behind and walk out into it.

Current admission

The National Trust currently lists the standard House, Park & Garden ticket at £19 adult and £9.50 child aged 5–17 without Gift Aid. A Park & Garden ticket is cheaper at £9 adult and £4.50 child. Under-fives and National Trust members are free.

Money-saving wrinkle: the National Trust currently gives free park-and-garden entry plus 10% off in its cafés if you arrive car-free and show a valid bus/train ticket or bike helmet.

Checked 25 September 2026

Two caveats worth knowing

House, garden and estate opening times vary through the year, so check the live page for your date rather than assuming one set of hours.

The usual northern Gritstone Trail route through Lyme currently has a short closure because of an unsafe bridge. Cheshire East and the National Trust have an alternative route in place. Follow current estate signs rather than an old GPX track.

Lyme also runs a volunteer-led shuttle from the entrance towards the house and gardens on many days, but it is subject to availability.
