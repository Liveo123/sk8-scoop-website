Before The Brew served wine, this Finney Lane address hosted Sunday school

217 Finney Lane has moved through a café, Methodist meetings, shops and cycle spares before becoming the family-run bar and coffee lounge now at 217A.

Checked2 OCT 2026Current business details checked against The Brew HG; earlier history against Heald Green Heritage.

The unexpected bit

A café became a temporary Methodist home

Heald Green Heritage records that Mr and Mrs Hargreaves, who owned a café at 217 Finney Lane, allowed a local Methodist congregation to use a room at the premises for services and Sunday school.

The first evening service there was held on 6 July 1930. Eight children attended Sunday school that day; by March 1931 the number had risen to 25.

The surviving account is not perfectly tidy about whether the room was behind, beside or adjoining the café. That uncertainty is worth keeping rather than pretending the historical evidence is more precise than it is.

Later uses

Florist, car and cycle spares, then The Brew

The Brew HG says the premises had previously been Going Dutch florist and, earlier, GT Car and Cycle Spares. The current family-run bar and coffee lounge opened in April 2021.

I haven’t been into The Brew, so this is not a review. The interesting part is the building history and the number of different local lives one small address has had.
