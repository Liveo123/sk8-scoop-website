Gatley Hall's industrial secret

A Grade II-listed Georgian house on Old Hall Road has an unlikely detail behind its grand frontage: a rear wing built for cotton manufacturing.

Last checked14 NOV 2026Part of SK8 Scoop Issue 21's website update.
A Georgian house with an industrial surprise

The factory behind Gatley Hall

There is more to Gatley Hall on Old Hall Road than its grand front entrance. Look at the official Grade II listing and an unusual detail appears: its two-storey rear wing was originally built as a cotton manufactory. So the property tells two stories at once, of domestic life and industrial work, long before the familiar streets of modern Gatley took shape.

It is tempting to imagine a noisy mill with tall chimneys, but that would go beyond the evidence. The listing confirms a manufactory wing, not its equipment, production output or workforce. The most interesting part may be what we still cannot establish.

What the official record actually establishes

Roscoe, 18th-century brickwork and a restored frontage

Historic England dates the main house to the mid-18th century and says it was built for William or Thomas Roscoe. The uncertainty in that wording matters: the record does not definitively identify which of them commissioned it.

The building has a four-bay plan, Flemish-bond brickwork and stone details. Its centre doorway has a decorated fanlight and columns, while the rear wing carries evidence of an entirely different purpose. Historic England records a restoration in 1972 and its Grade II listing dates to 4 March 1970.

The unanswered questions

Who worked behind that door?

What cotton processes took place there? Who worked in the wing, and how were they paid? When did the activity cease? Neither the statutory listing nor the council's heritage entry answers those questions. A locally researched account mentions a 1791 sales notice, but we have not verified the original advertisement, so it cannot yet pin down a working date.

These are proper research questions, not a licence to invent a Victorian factory scene. Further archival records could change our understanding of the site.

Before you go

Research it without visiting the property

The listing gives the address as 13 Old Hall Road, Gatley, SK8 4BE. This is private property, not a visitor attraction. Please do not enter its grounds or assume public access.

The council's heritage entry provides a photograph, and the Historic England register contains the documented architecture. Both can be consulted online.
