Why this one earns its place

Beneath Stockport town centre is a sandstone tunnel network dug as a civilian air-raid shelter. It is one of those local facts that sounds slightly invented until you are actually walking underground.

The best reason to go is not a checklist of exhibits. It is the physical experience of the narrow passages, wartime fittings and the odd feeling of standing below streets you normally cross without giving the ground beneath them much thought.

Checked 25 September 2026

Current prices and opening times

Stockport Council currently lists adult admission at £6 and concessions for people aged 60+ and students at £4.50. Under-16s are free with a paying adult. Leisure Key holders and companions of registered disabled visitors are also listed as free.

Current standard opening is Tuesday to Friday, 1pm–5pm; Saturday, 10am–5pm; and Sunday, 11am–4pm. Monday and bank holidays are listed as closed. Last entry is one hour before closing.

Before you go

Underground means a few extra checks

Check the live Council page before travelling, particularly if access needs or claustrophobia could affect the visit. Underground heritage spaces can be awkward in ways that a modern museum is not.

If you want to turn it into a fuller Stockport day, the shelters are close enough to the Old Town and Underbanks to make a second stop easy.
