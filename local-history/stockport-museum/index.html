STOCKPORT MUSEUM — MARKET PLACE

A free wander through Stockport’s story, from prehistoric finds and medieval life to industry, everyday objects and the town people recognise today. It is the sort of museum where you can dip in for 45 minutes or stay longer reading the odd local detail.

Good for: rainy days, families, adults, pairing with the Underbanks or Market.

Checked 25 September 2026

Current opening

Stockport Council currently lists free entry. Opening is Tuesday to Saturday, 10am–5pm, and Sunday, 11am–4pm. Monday and bank holidays are closed.

Food and drink are not allowed in the galleries, but Market Place has cafés and places to eat nearby.
