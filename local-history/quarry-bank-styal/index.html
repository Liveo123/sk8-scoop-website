What current admission covers

The National Trust’s 2026/27 prices currently list standard admission without Gift Aid at £24 adult and £12 child aged 5–17, with under-fives and National Trust members free.

The admission covers the Mill, Apprentice House, Quarry Bank House, Walled Garden, Cliff Gardens and Tennis Lawn unless stated otherwise.

Good for: families, adults, industrial-history readers and anyone who wants the machinery and the social history in the same visit.

Checked 25 September 2026

Guided access matters

Visits to the Apprentice House and Worker’s Cottage are guided rather than simply open for wandering. The National Trust says to ask at Visitor Reception for Apprentice House tour tickets.

Opening times vary by part of the site, so check the live day-specific timetable before deciding which order to do the mill, houses and gardens.
