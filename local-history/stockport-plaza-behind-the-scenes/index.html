52 Adventures · Stockport town centre

Get behind the curtain at Stockport Plaza.

A normal visit shows you the auditorium. The guided tour is the version that gets you into the building’s story.

AllowABOUT 1½ HRSTicketed. Tours run on selected dates rather than every day.
Why it earns an adventure slot

The building is the attraction.

Rainy-day choice
Opened1932

The Plaza describes itself as an Art Deco Super Cinema and Variety Theatre, and the tour is built around seeing parts of that building you do not normally reach from a seat.

Tour formatGuided access

The current tour listings describe a guided look behind the scenes, with proceeds supporting the theatre’s maintenance and restoration appeal.

AccessStairs are part of the standard route

The Plaza asks visitors with access needs to contact the venue in advance so adaptations can be discussed.

From 52 Adventures

Why not just go to a show?

You can see the main auditorium at a performance. The point of the tour is the extra access: guided history and spaces that turn the building itself into the day out.

Good for: adults, families, theatre fans and anyone who notices old cinemas, staircases, signage and backstage machinery.
