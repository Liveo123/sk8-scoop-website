How history pages should work

Primary and official sources should carry the factual spine. Local recollections and secondary sources can add colour, but they should not quietly become hard fact just because they make the story better.

Permanent pages earn their keep by holding the evidence. The newsletter can stay readable and lively while the website keeps source notes, corrections and deeper context.
