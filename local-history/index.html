The Finney Lane thread

Heald Green Heritage records that the Dutton family divided their bungalow near the hotel and rented one half to Jack and Marthe McKenna. It identifies Marthe as the Belgian nurse and First World War spy whose memoir became I Was a Spy.

The hotel thread

Heald Green Heritage says Tony Townsend, the hotel licensees’ son, confirmed that Guy Gibson and around 40 RAF men used the hotel’s upstairs Committee Room during the war. We keep that labelled as a local recorded account rather than silently upgrading it to a primary-source fact.

The story improves when the labels stay honest

Official or primary evidence should carry hard facts. Local research and recollections can add valuable colour. Anything unresolved stays visibly unresolved, even when a neater version would make a punchier anecdote.
