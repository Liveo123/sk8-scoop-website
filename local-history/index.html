An 11th-century cross inside

Historic England records an 11th-century cross inside St Mary’s. Most of the church people see today is 16th century, so this one object reaches several centuries further back.

The medieval cross outside became a sundial

East of the church is a separate medieval cross shaft, later adapted as a sundial. It is another layer of Cheadle history sitting in a place many people pass routinely.
