Local history / archive clue · Gatley · Checked 20 November 2026

The pub at the end of Gatley’s tram line

Outside the Horse and Farrier, a c.1905 postcard records Gatley’s tram terminus. The same junction is still recognisable today.

Next time you pass the Horse & Farrier on Gatley Road, imagine a tram pulled up beside the kerb, with children and adults watching the photographer.

That is what Stockport Image Archive's image 23329 shows. Its catalogue identifies the scene as The Horse and Farrier Public House, Gatley, with an electric tram at the terminus. It dates the postcard to approximately 1905.

Stand near the pub and look east along Gatley Road towards Cheadle. That is the direction the tram travelled back towards Stockport.

The archive credits no known photographer for this image. Rather than reproduce it without a separately recorded image licence, we are linking directly to the original archive record, where you can examine the scene yourself.
