52 Adventures · Buxworth

Explore an inland port at Bugsworth Basin.

The trick is to stop seeing a quiet canal basin and start reconstructing the huge limestone port that once operated here.

Core costFREELimited parking on site. Volunteer facilities have limited hours.
Why it is worth the trip

Industrial archaeology you can walk through.

Allow 1½–2½ hours
ThenMajor inland port

Canal & River Trust describes Bugsworth as once the largest and busiest inland port on the narrow canal system.

NowBasins, channels and surviving structures

The layout is still readable on foot, which is what makes the site more useful than a simple waterside stop.

Extra layerSmall museum + welcome station

These volunteer-run facilities add context when open, but should not be the sole reason for travelling.

From 52 Adventures

Walk it slowly

Try to reconstruct how limestone arrived, where boats loaded and how workers moved between the wharves. The surviving shape of the basin does most of the storytelling if you give it time.

Good for: families, canal walkers and industrial-history readers.

Checked 25 September 2026

Current visitor details

Canal & River Trust currently lists the basin as free apart from parking. The welcome station, museum and children’s activity area are normally open Wednesdays, Fridays and weekends from 10am to 2pm, but those volunteer hours are explicitly subject to change.

Parking on site is limited. Whaley Bridge railway station and the 199 bus route are both a short walk away along the canal arm.

The toilets normally open when volunteers are on site and can also be accessed with a Canal & River Trust key at other times.
