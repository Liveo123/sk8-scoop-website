Current visitor details

Stockport Council currently lists hall admission at £7 adults and £5.50 concessions; under-16s are free with a paying adult. The hall is currently open Thursday to Sunday, 11am–4:30pm.

The Solar remains closed because of roof restoration works. Guided tours run at 2pm on Fridays and Sundays, subject to availability, and currently cost £10 per person. Parking is limited, especially at busy times.
