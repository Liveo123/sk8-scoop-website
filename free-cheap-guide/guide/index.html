How prices, schedules and accessibility notes are handled

FREE means the core participation or admission is free. Parking, transport, food and optional extras may still cost money; where those extras materially change the outing, we say so.

Group-priced activities are shown with the real group or session total first. Any per-person number is an example to help you judge value rather than the main price claim.

Accessibility and SEND-related notes are included only where we have evidence for the specific claim. We would rather say less than guess.

Prices, opening hours and recurring schedules change. The permanent guide is rechecked periodically, while What’s On Now is designed to be refreshed more often.

Museums, heritage & landmarks

★
IF YOU ONLY PICK ONE

Stockport Air Raid Shelters for the most distinctive paid option; Hat Works for the strongest completely free indoor visit.

AVRO HERITAGE MUSEUM — WOODFORD

UNDER £10
Adult £9 • concession £7 • age 11–16 £5 • under-11s free

Walk into the old Woodford aerodrome and you are in the middle of Avro’s story: aircraft, engines, cockpit sections, photographs, uniforms and the people who designed and built some of Britain’s most famous planes. It feels more like exploring a working aviation story than looking at a few objects in cases, and there is enough here to keep aircraft fans and curious families occupied for a proper visit.

GOOD FORfamilies, aviation fans, adults, older children.
WHENFriday to Sunday, plus Bank Holiday Mondays, 10am–4pm.
£
WATCH THE EXTRAS

optional cockpit tours cost extra.

HAT WORKS — STOCKPORT

OUTSIDE SK8 • RAINY DAY • WORTH THE TRIP
FREE

General museum admission is free.
This is not just a room full of hats. Hat Works takes you through Stockport’s hatting story with original machinery, recreated workshop spaces, displays of finished hats and the people behind the industry. You can see how felt hats were made, why the trade mattered so much locally and, if you pay for the separate factory-floor tour, get much closer to the machinery and manufacturing process.

GOOD FORrainy days, adults, older children, local-history fans.
WHENThursday–Saturday, 10am–4pm; last admission 3.15pm. Closed Sunday–Wednesday.
£
WATCH THE EXTRAS

factory-floor guided tours are separate — currently £8.50 full price and £7 concessions, plus booking fee.

STOCKPORT MUSEUM — MARKET PLACE

OUTSIDE SK8 • RAINY DAY
FREE

A free wander through Stockport’s story, from prehistoric finds and medieval life to industry, everyday objects and the town people recognise today. It is the sort of museum where you can dip in for 45 minutes or stay longer reading the odd local detail, and its Market Place location makes it easy to combine with the Underbanks, Hat Works or the Market.

GOOD FORrainy days, families, adults, pairing with the Underbanks or Market.
WHENTuesday–Saturday 10am–5pm; Sunday 11am–4pm. Closed Mondays and Bank Holidays.
PARKINGnearest council car parks charge; Stockport bus and railway stations are a short walk away.

STOCKPORT AIR RAID SHELTERS

OUTSIDE SK8 • JUST FOR FUN • RAINY DAY • WORTH THE TRIP
UNDER £10
Adult £6 • concession £4.50 • under-16s free with a paying adult

Head underground into the sandstone tunnels dug beneath Stockport during the Second World War. You move through shelter passages, sleeping areas and reconstructed wartime spaces while learning what it was like for local people to spend air raids below the town. The setting does most of the work here: it feels like going somewhere unusual, not simply reading another wartime display.

GOOD FORolder children, adults, history fans, rainy days.
WHENTuesday–Friday, 1–5pm; Saturday, 10am–5pm; Sunday, 11am–4pm. Closed Monday and Bank Holidays. Last admission is one hour before closing.

STAIRCASE HOUSE — STOCKPORT MARKET PLACE

OUTSIDE SK8 • Pair it with
UNDER £10
Adult £6 • concession £4.50 • under-16s free with a paying adult

Step inside a house that has been altered, lived in and added to for centuries. Rooms are dressed to show different periods of domestic life, and the star is the remarkable Jacobean cage-newel staircase winding through the centre of the building. It works particularly well as part of a Market Place/Underbanks wander because you go from the modern town straight into a very different version of Stockport.

GOOD FORadults, older children, local history, pairing with the Market or Underbanks.
WHENWednesday–Thursday, 10am–5pm; Friday, noon–5pm; Saturday, 10am–5pm; Sunday, 11am–4pm. Closed Monday, Tuesday and Bank Holidays. Last admission is 1 hour 15 minutes before closing.

BRAMALL HALL — BRAMHALL

OUTSIDE SK8 • WORTH THE TRIP • Half-day
UNDER £10
Adult £7 • concession £5.50 • under-16s free with a paying adult

A timber-framed Tudor manor house where the pleasure is as much in wandering through the rooms as in reading the labels: carved wood, period interiors, old staircases, painted decoration and stories about the families who lived there. The surrounding parkland means you can turn a museum visit into a longer, slower outing without paying for another attraction.

GOOD FORfamilies, adults, history fans.
WHENThursday–Sunday, 11am–4.30pm for the Hall; closed Monday–Wednesday and Bank Holidays. Occasional wedding closures: check before travelling.

EXPLORE STOCKPORT’S UNDERBANKS

OUTSIDE SK8 • Quick visit • Pair it with
FREE

Treat the Underbanks as a free urban treasure hunt: steep little streets, old shopfronts, carved stonework, alleyways, painted walls and dozens of listed buildings packed into a compact part of town. There is no ticket or formal route to follow, so it works well if you like nosing around at your own pace, stopping for photos or pairing it with the Market and museums.

GOOD FORadults, couples, photography, an hour or two, pairing with the Market or museums.
COSTfree to explore; spending is entirely optional.

MANCHESTER MUSEUM

OUTSIDE SK8 • WORTH THE TRIP • RAINY DAY • Half-day
FREE

This is the big free museum day out: dinosaurs and fossils, ancient Egypt and archaeology, animals, world cultures and changing displays spread across a large university museum. It is especially useful when you need a weather-proof half-day because there is enough variety for different ages to split up, explore and regroup without everyone having to be interested in the same thing.

GOOD FORfamilies, rainy days, older children, half-day trips.

SCIENCE AND INDUSTRY MUSEUM — MANCHESTER

OUTSIDE SK8 • WORTH THE TRIP • RAINY DAY • Half-day
FREE

Big machines, engines, transport, textiles, computing and the story of how Manchester helped drive the Industrial Revolution. The restored Power Hall is now open again, bringing working engines and refreshed displays back into the free visit alongside the museum’s other permanent galleries. The wider historic site remains part of a long-term conservation programme, so access to individual areas can still change.

GOOD FORfamilies, older children, rainy days, half-day trips.
WHENopen daily, 10am–5pm.
£
WATCH THE EXTRAS

general museum admission and the Power Hall are free; some special exhibitions and experiences are ticketed separately.

JOHN RYLANDS LIBRARY — MANCHESTER

OUTSIDE SK8 • WORTH THE TRIP • Quick visit
FREE

The building is the attraction as much as the collection. You walk through dramatic neo-Gothic corridors, vaulted reading rooms, stone arches and dark wood interiors before reaching displays drawn from rare books, manuscripts and historic collections. It is an easy one-hour Manchester stop that feels grander and stranger than a normal library visit.

GOOD FORadults, couples, older children, architecture fans, rainy days.
WHENWednesday–Saturday, 10am–5pm. No booking required for general admission.

GATLEY CARRS LOCAL NATURE RESERVE

FREE

Woodland, meadow, ponds and winding paths squeezed into Gatley, with enough birds, water, trees and rougher edges to feel like a small nature reserve rather than a tidy municipal park. It is good for an unhurried wander, a picnic or giving children something natural to investigate without driving out into the countryside.

GOOD FORfamilies, adults, picnics, dog walks, short nature outings.
WHENPlan a daylight visit; the main car park gate is locked at dusk.
