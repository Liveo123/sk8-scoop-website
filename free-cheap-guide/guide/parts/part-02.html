BRAMALL HALL — BRAMHALL

OUTSIDE SK8 • WORTH THE TRIP • Half-day
UNDER £10
Adult £7 • concession £5.50 • under-16s free with a paying adult
A timber-framed Tudor manor house where the pleasure is as much in wandering through the rooms as in reading the labels: carved wood, period interiors, old staircases, painted decoration and stories about the families who lived there. The surrounding parkland means you can turn a museum visit into a longer, slower outing without paying for another attraction.
Good for: families, adults, history fans.
Watch the current works: the Solar is closed and some displays are affected by roof restoration, so check before travelling.

eligible Leisure Key holders can currently enter free.

EXPLORE STOCKPORT’S UNDERBANKS

OUTSIDE SK8 • Quick visit • Pair it with
FREE
Treat the Underbanks as a free urban treasure hunt: steep little streets, old shopfronts, carved stonework, alleyways, painted walls and dozens of listed buildings packed into a compact part of town. There is no ticket or formal route to follow, so it works well if you like nosing around at your own pace, stopping for photos or pairing it with the Market and museums.

SCIENCE AND INDUSTRY MUSEUM — MANCHESTER

OUTSIDE SK8 • WORTH THE TRIP • RAINY DAY • Half-day
FREE
Big machines, engines, transport, textiles, computing and the story of how Manchester helped drive the Industrial Revolution. The appeal is scale: there are things to look up at, walk around and puzzle over rather than just rows of display cases. Because parts of the historic site are being restored, the exact experience can change, but the free permanent galleries still make this a strong rainy-day trip.
Good for: families, older children, rainy days, half-day trips.

some special experiences and exhibitions are ticketed, and restoration means parts of the site can change.

JOHN RYLANDS LIBRARY — MANCHESTER

OUTSIDE SK8 • WORTH THE TRIP • Quick visit
FREE
The building is the attraction as much as the collection. You walk through dramatic neo-Gothic corridors, vaulted reading rooms, stone arches and dark wood interiors before reaching displays drawn from rare books, manuscripts and historic collections. It is an easy one-hour Manchester stop that feels grander and stranger than a normal library visit.
Good for: adults, couples, older children, architecture fans, rainy days.
When: Wednesday–Saturday, 10am–5pm. No booking required for general admission.

THE WHITWORTH — MANCHESTER

OUTSIDE SK8 • WORTH THE TRIP • Pair it with
FREE
The Whitworth mixes modern and historic art, textiles, wallpapers and changing exhibitions in a gallery that opens directly into Whitworth Park. You can move from indoor galleries to lawns and trees without making a second journey, and the family programme regularly adds hands-on art, outdoor sessions and creative activities that make the trip feel less formal than a traditional gallery.
Good for: adults, families, rainy days with an outdoor escape if the weather behaves.
Why it makes the cut: it is one of the few wider-Manchester arts trips strong enough to justify the extra journey from SK8.

ABNEY HALL PARK — CHEADLE

FREE
A surprisingly varied patch of Cheadle: old parkland, woodland paths, wetland, open grass and little changes of scenery that make a short local walk feel more substantial. You can do a quick loop, let children burn off energy, take the dog or simply wander without needing to turn it into a full day out.
Good for: short walks, families, dog walks, an hour or two.
