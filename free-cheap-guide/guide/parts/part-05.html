One Stop Away

Take a local bus or train for exactly one stop, get off, then walk back using public pavements and paths. The mission is to spot three things you normally miss from the window: one old thing, one useful thing and one odd detail worth looking up later.

Cost: walking is free; normal travel fare may apply unless your pass or ticket already covers the journey.
Best for: turning an ordinary local journey into a 30–60 minute mini expedition.
Before you go: check the walking route and return options first, go in daylight and avoid private or uncertain paths.
