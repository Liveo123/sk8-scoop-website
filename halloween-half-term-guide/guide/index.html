What is happening when?

This is a quick planning view, not a substitute for each event card. Multi-day activities appear in the most useful starting window.

10–18 October

  • Reddish Vale Pumpkin Festival starts 10 Oct
  • Gandeys starts 10 Oct
  • Reddish Vale midweek offer: 12–14 Oct
  • Moonlight Pumpkins: 17 Oct
  • Macclesfield Apple Day: 18 Oct

19–25 October

  • Reddish Vale midweek offer: 19–21 Oct
  • Major museum programmes and trails begin 24 Oct
  • Wallace & Gromit: 24–25 Oct
  • Halloween in the City: 24–25 Oct
  • Moonlight Pumpkins: 24–25 Oct
  • Not Now, Bernard and Halloween in Concert: 25 Oct

26–30 October

  • OCTOBERFEST holiday club: Mon–Fri
  • K-Pop Dance Heroes and Aardman Claystations: 26 Oct
  • Whitworth programme: 28–29 Oct; Afrocats page lists 28–30 Oct
  • Claymation, Magic Makers and Tatton workshops: 29 Oct
  • Music Makers, Pumpkin Path and Sanderson Sisters: 30 Oct

31 October–1 November

  • Arc Saturday Art Club: Halloween
  • Dayglo Family Rave: Halloween
  • Sanderson Sisters: Halloween
  • Dunham, Quarry Bank, Jodrell, museums and trails continue through 1 Nov
  • Spooky Plants ends 1 Nov

Date warning: the Whitworth’s Afrocats page prints “Tuesday 28–Thursday 30 October”, but those weekdays do not match the 2026 calendar. This guide uses the numerical dates and tells readers to recheck.
