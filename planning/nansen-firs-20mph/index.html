A new 20mph zone is proposed for the Nansen/Firs Road estate

Consultation opened 3 September and closes 24 September 2026.

Stockport Council has opened a consultation on a proposed 20mph speed-limit zone across the Nansen/Firs Road estate in Heald Green. The council says the proposal is part of its wider road-safety strategy and is intended to make residential streets safer while supporting walking, wheeling and cycling.

The consultation opened Thursday 3 September and closes Thursday 24 September. If you live on or regularly use the estate, this is the fresh Heald Green traffic proposal to look at this week.

Key date

Closes 24 September 2026
