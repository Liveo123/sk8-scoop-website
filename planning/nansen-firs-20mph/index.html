Nansen / Firs 20mph proposal

The useful version of the consultation: what is proposed, why the council says it is considering it, and the date by which comments are due.

Consultation file

Three things that matter before you open the paperwork.

Heald Green · Live consultation
01 · ProposalA 20mph speed-limit zone

Stockport Council is consulting on a proposed 20mph speed-limit zone for the Nansen/Firs Road Estate residential area.

02 · Council rationaleRoad safety and active travel

The council says the area has been identified as one where a reduced speed limit could provide road-safety benefits and support walking, wheeling and cycling.

03 · Response window3 to 24 September 2026

The proposal is still open for comments during that window. Use the official consultation page for the full scheme material and response route.

SK8 Scoop rule: this page explains the proposal, not whether you should support or oppose it. The official consultation remains the authoritative source for the scheme documents and response process.

What it means in plain English

A lower proposed speed limit across the estate

Stockport Council says it has a policy of introducing 20mph speed limits within residential areas as part of its wider road-safety approach. For the Nansen/Firs Road Estate, it is asking for views on applying a 20mph zone within the residential area.

The council links the proposal to road safety and to making walking, wheeling and cycling feel safer. That is the council’s stated rationale for the consultation, rather than a conclusion SK8 Scoop is adding independently.

The practical bit is the deadline: the consultation opened on 3 September and closes on 24 September 2026. If you want to respond, use the council’s consultation hub so you are working from the current scheme material.
