Three Heald Green housing allocations in the new Local Plan

The September 2026 Regulation 19 plan identifies three substantial housing allocations around Heald Green. This is a policy stage, not planning permission for all of the homes.

First, the important distinction

This does not grant planning permission

A Local Plan allocates land and sets policies that guide later planning decisions. Individual development proposals still need to go through the planning system. That distinction matters because a headline such as “790 homes” can otherwise sound as though construction has already been approved.

What happens next

The next consultation is about whether the plan is legally compliant and sound

Stockport Council says the next consultation is on the version of the Local Plan it proposes to publish before submission to an independent Planning Inspector. At this stage, people will be able to comment on whether the plan has been prepared in line with legal requirements and whether it can be considered sound.

Issue 13’s verified production record gives the planned consultation window as 23 September to 8 November 2026. The council says the participation details and response route will be published on its Local Plan page when available, so check that page before trying to submit a response.

Do not confuse two different things

Heald Green East appears in both policy and planning discussions

The Local Plan allocation for Heald Green East is listed at 790 homes. Separately, planning application DC/095134 is an outline application for up to 675 homes on land north of Stanley Road and west of the A34. They are connected geographically, but they are not the same decision or the same number.
