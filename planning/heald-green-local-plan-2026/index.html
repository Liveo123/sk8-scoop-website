Four proposed housing sites in Heald Green's draft Local Plan

What changed

The current draft lists four Heald Green housing allocations

The September 2026 draft identifies Heald Green East at 790, Heald Green West at 750, Bolshaw Road at 64, and Daisy Bank at 132 homes. Our earlier count omitted Bolshaw Road; we have corrected that error.

Added together, that is 1,736 proposed homes across four allocations. These figures are indicative capacities, not approved homes.

Important distinction

Local Plan allocation does not mean planning permission

The Local Plan identifies land and policies that would guide future planning decisions and applications. A site being allocated in the plan does not mean all of those homes have been granted planning permission.

That distinction matters because the Regulation 19 stage is about the plan itself, including whether it has been prepared in line with legal requirements and whether it can be considered sound.

Key dates

The Regulation 19 consultation period has ended

Stockport Council's consultation began in September 2026 and its published response period ended in November. These draft documents remain useful but the old response deadline has passed.

The draft will continue through the formal plan-making process. Check Stockport Council for subsequent stages and any separate planning applications.
