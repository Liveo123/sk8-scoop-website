Three Heald Green housing allocations in the final Local Plan consultation

The Regulation 19 Local Plan proposes 1,672 homes across Heald Green East, Heald Green West and Daisy Bank. This is a policy stage, not planning permission.

What changed

The final draft now names three substantial Heald Green allocations

Stockport’s Regulation 19 Local Plan includes Heald Green East at 790 homes, Heald Green West at 750 and Daisy Bank at 132.

Added together, that is 1,672 homes across the three proposed allocations.

Important distinction

Local Plan allocation does not mean planning permission

The Local Plan identifies land and policies that would guide future planning decisions and applications. A site being allocated in the plan does not mean all of those homes have been granted planning permission.

That distinction matters because the Regulation 19 stage is about the plan itself, including whether it has been prepared in line with legal requirements and whether it can be considered sound.

Key dates

Consultation is due to run from 23 September to 8 November

Stockport Council’s current Local Plan page says the Regulation 19 consultation is due to run from Wednesday 23 September to Sunday 8 November 2026.

The council says final participation details and the response route will be published on its Local Plan pages when available. If one of the proposed sites affects you, this is the page to bookmark and recheck once the consultation opens.
