How SK8 Scoop reads a consultation

Four things before the paperwork gets interesting.

Change · Place · Deadline · Source
01 · ChangeWhat is actually proposed?

Start with the concrete change rather than the policy language.

02 · PlaceWhere does it apply?

Pull out the streets, neighbourhoods and sites readers can recognise.

03 · DeadlineCan you still respond?

Keep the closing date prominent and update the status after it passes.

04 · SourceWhere is the official detail?

Every live consultation should point back to the council’s current page or form.
