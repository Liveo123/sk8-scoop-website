How SK8 Scoop reads a consultation

Four things before the paperwork gets interesting.

Change · Place · Deadline · Source
01 · ChangeWhat is actually proposed?

Speed limit, parking restriction, crossing, development or another concrete change.

02 · PlaceWhere does it apply?

The street, estate or junction comes before the policy language.

03 · DeadlineCan you still respond?

The cards keep the closing date visible and change state after it passes.

04 · SourceWhere is the official detail?

Every card points back to the council page or consultation hub.

Useful rule: closed consultations stay visibly closed rather than vanishing, because the proposal may still matter after the response window ends.
