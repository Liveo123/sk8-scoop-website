Stockport Local Plan: the Regulation 19 consultation has closed

Read the council's draft documents →

The consultation that ran during September–November 2026 is over. The draft policies and supporting documents remain available. A draft housing allocation does not itself grant permission for development.

Heald Green's current draft includes four allocations totalling 1,736 proposed homes. Daisy Bank has a proposed heritage-retention requirement. The next useful step is to understand the policies and watch for subsequent formal decisions, rather than try to submit a late response.
