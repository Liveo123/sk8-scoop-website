Heald Green East: what the 675-home application actually proposes

A large outline application sits on land north of Stanley Road and west of the A34. Here is the useful version: scale, status, what “outline” means, and which extras are proposals rather than promises.

What is on the register?

The application in plain English

Planning application DC/095134 concerns Heald Green East, on land north of Stanley Road and west of the A34. The registered description is for an extension to the existing urban settlement, delivering up to 675 homes, public open space and recreation areas, Class C2 and Class E development, and associated infrastructure.

The word “outline” is important. This is not the same as approving the final appearance of 675 finished houses. An outline application establishes broad development principles while reserving detailed matters for later applications.

SK8 Scoop first covered the proposal in Issue 4 in July 2026. At that point it had not been decided. The latest public planning trackers checked on 17 September still list DC/095134 as pending, despite an earlier expected decision date having passed. The council’s planning database remains the place to check the live status.

What else is in the masterplan?

Useful ideas to note, but do not confuse them with delivered infrastructure

Bloor Homes’ consultation material describes a wider masterplan including green space, play areas, walking and cycling routes, a local centre that could contain shops or health facilities, and land safeguarded for possible future SEND facilities.

The developer also shows safeguarded land for a potential future park-and-ride facility and railway station. Its own material says those transport elements would depend on other bodies such as Network Rail or Transport for Greater Manchester. They should therefore not be read as confirmed parts of a timetable.

That distinction matters on a scheme this large: an item appearing on a masterplan is not the same thing as funding, approval or delivery being secured.

How to check it yourself

Use the reference number, not a social-media screenshot

Stockport Council’s planning database is the authoritative place for new documents and any decision. Search for DC/095134.
