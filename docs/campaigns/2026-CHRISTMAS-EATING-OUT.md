# Christmas Eating Out 2026 advertiser campaign

Status: supervised pilot specification. No external outreach or publication is authorised by this document.

## 1. Job to be done

Help suitable local restaurants, pubs and venues generate festive booking interest from SK8 readers while testing the SK8 Scoop for Business acquisition-to-results workflow.

Primary advertiser outcomes:
- table bookings
- festive menu bookings
- party/group enquiries
- Christmas Day bookings where applicable
- bookings for a specific seasonal event where applicable

Primary reader action: **Book** or **Enquire**.

## 2. Why this is the first pilot

The need is concrete and time-bound. Businesses can promote a real menu, event, group-booking proposition or booking window rather than vague brand awareness. A table/group booking can also carry enough value for a small paid test to be commercially plausible.

The pilot must not imply that SK8 has already proven Christmas-booking ROI. It has not.

## 3. Geography

Priority order:
1. Cheadle
2. Cheadle Hulme
3. Gatley
4. Heald Green
5. immediately nearby only where reader relevance is genuinely strong

Do not widen to generic Stockport hospitality merely to increase prospect volume.

## 4. Qualifying trigger signals

Priority rises when a business has a verified current signal such as:

- Christmas/festive bookings now open
- festive menu live
- Christmas Day availability
- party/group/private dining offer
- Christmas event or seasonal experience
- truthful early-booking offer
- a specific booking deadline or limited capacity

Re-check the trigger immediately before any outreach.

## 5. Commercial fit score

100-point score:

- core SK8 geography: 20
- live festive commercial trigger: 20
- strong reader relevance: 15
- meaningful booking/customer value: 15
- specific promotable proposition: 10
- clear booking/enquiry route: 10
- strong timing: 5
- existing SK8 relationship/evidence: 5

Action bands:
- 80-100: priority supervised outreach
- 65-79: worthwhile if capacity remains
- 50-64: reserve
- below 50: do not spend active sales time

Legal/contact eligibility remains a separate gate. A high commercial score never overrides PECR/GDPR/contact-route rules or a suppression record.

## 6. Current offer

Default temporary product: **TEST £40**.

TEST should contain:
- one clearly labelled sponsored newsletter placement
- one festive booking proposition
- one primary CTA
- SK8-created paid creative from verified facts
- advertiser factual/brand approval
- appropriate tracked destination link
- short evidence review after publication
- no automatic renewal

Do **not** force GROW £90 for ordinary restaurant Christmas bookings. Use GROW only if the actual proposition is a genuine Free & Cheap Guide fit under the current approved temporary system.

## 7. Recommendation logic

### Recommend TEST when
- there is a clear festive offer/menu/event;
- one primary booking/enquiry action exists;
- timing is suitable for a newsletter placement;
- the business is a good SK8 fit;
- there is no evidence that extra surfaces are needed.

### Recommend GROW only when
- the same advertiser proposition genuinely belongs in the Free & Cheap Guide;
- newsletter + Guide create two useful contexts around the same objective;
- the Guide placement is currently deliverable and available.

### Recommend “fix this first” when
- booking route is unclear/broken;
- festive proposition is too vague;
- dates/prices/terms cannot be verified;
- there is no sensible reader action.

### Recommend no paid campaign when
- economics are implausible for the proposition;
- the advertiser is outside useful geography/relevance;
- timing is already too late;
- suitability/trust/compliance concerns are material.

## 8. Acquisition routes

Use the same campaign hook across channels:

**“Christmas bookings are already opening. If you run a suitable local venue, tell SK8 Scoop what you most want to fill and we’ll suggest the smallest sensible route.”**

Distribution order for pilot:
1. supervised trigger-based outreach to a very small qualified batch;
2. SK8-owned advertiser CTA when approved for publication;
3. direct referrals/intros from suitable existing contacts;
4. useful public opportunity/resource page after the enquiry flow is confirmed working;
5. search/long-tail discovery over time.

Do not start paid acquisition until request-to-campaign conversion is known.

## 9. First-contact objective

The first message does not need to close £40 immediately. It should earn a small useful response, for example permission to send the suggested route or a link to the short advertiser page.

Avoid attachments, media-pack dumps, unsupported audience claims and calls requested in the first message unless the prospect invites one.

No live outreach is authorised until the first batch is reviewed by Paul.

## 10. Example message structure

Not send-ready until prospect facts/contact eligibility are verified:

- specific current festive hook
- why that hook could suit local SK8 readers
- one sentence explaining the small TEST route
- SK8 does the creative work
- one easy reply CTA
- clear SK8 identity and opt-out route where required

Keep the message compact and business-specific.

## 11. Booking flow

Prospect/visitor -> short goal-first enquiry -> D1 inbound record -> check against main CRM -> suitability/availability review -> best-fit recommendation -> scope/timing confirmed in writing -> Stripe invoice/payment route -> creative facts/assets -> SK8 draft -> advertiser factual approval -> Paul publication approval -> schedule.

No payment at first enquiry.

## 12. Campaign ID

Use the established readable convention:

`YYYY-MM-business-shortname-test`

Example format only: `2026-10-examplevenue-test`.

Preserve the same ID through proposal, tracking, results and renewal records.

## 13. Creative standard

The paid placement should answer quickly:

1. Why should an SK8 reader care now?
2. What exactly is available?
3. What practical date/price/terms matter?
4. What should they do next?

One primary CTA. Clearly label the unit as paid/sponsored before promotional content. Do not mimic editorial recommendation language or badges.

## 14. Tracking

Tracked destination should use campaign-specific UTM parameters where the destination tolerates them:

- utm_source=sk8_scoop
- utm_medium=newsletter_sponsored
- utm_campaign=<campaign_id>
- utm_content=<placement identifier>

Record the exact final destination before publication.

## 15. Success measurement

Before publication record the advertiser objective in plain English.

Afterwards separate:

### SK8-measured
- delivered/exposed measure only where meaningful and available
- tracked clicks/interactions

### Advertiser-confirmed
- enquiries
- bookings
- group/party bookings
- revenue only if voluntarily and reliably supplied

### Commercial
- advertiser feedback
- repeat/change/stop decision
- renewal/next opportunity
- time spent delivering the sale/campaign

Unknown stays unknown.

## 16. Pilot learning questions

The pilot should answer:

1. Can we find good prospects without mass cold email?
2. Which contact route produces useful replies?
3. Does the £40 TEST proposition make sense to hospitality businesses?
4. How much Paul time does one campaign consume?
5. Do advertisers understand the goal-first proposition without a media pack?
6. What proportion of enquiries reach approved/payment stage?
7. Can we obtain credible downstream booking feedback?
8. Is the next sensible hospitality opportunity obvious enough to support retention?

## 17. Stop / review rules

Review rather than scale if:
- outreach generates objections/complaints;
- qualified businesses repeatedly misunderstand the proposition;
- reply-to-paid conversion is too weak to justify prospecting time;
- creative/admin workload makes £40 uneconomic;
- advertiser feedback suggests the placement is not useful;
- measurement cannot support a sensible repeat/change/stop decision.

Do not solve weak economics by automating more outreach.

## 18. Current readiness gate before first outreach

Must be true:

- current advertiser page and enquiry form are working;
- enquiry lands in D1;
- Paul has a reliable way to see new enquiries;
- notification route is working or there is a checked fallback queue;
- current TEST £40 wording remains the approved operating offer;
- main CRM is checked for duplicates and suppression;
- each prospect has a fresh trigger and lawful/appropriate contact route;
- first outreach batch has Paul approval.

Only then send the supervised pilot batch.
