# Indoor play — scoring evidence and next verification

Use the 100-point framework (30 play experience, 20 value, 15 family practical experience, 10 parent experience, 10 inclusion, 10 review consistency, 5 distinctiveness). Establish a clear category-specific basis and note the evidence source/check date; never award or deduct points merely because information is unknown. A final score needs sufficient evidence for all material dimensions, not a mean of review-site ratings. Review evidence needs a transparent dated recent sample, positive and critical reports, and acknowledgement of sample/selection bias. Venue-provided facilities are claims, not independent observations. Publish the score only after editorial review. Eight research picks are provisional, not winners of a scored league table.
