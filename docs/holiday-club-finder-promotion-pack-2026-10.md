## Ten focused critique passes
1. Problem: a specific time-sensitive childcare query, not a generic website launch.
2. Location: nine locations are nearby, so always say 'in and around SK8', not '18 in SK8'.
3. Date: programmes vary within 26–30 October; do not imply every club runs all five days.
4. Evidence: cite organiser bookings where available, and qualify restricted ClassForKids access.
5. Price: never reintroduce the conflicting Practically Family old price claims.
6. Vacancy: no available-space guarantee; verify again before share.
7. Privacy: do not collect a child's age via analytics; existing tracking is consent-gated.
8. Editorial trust: organisations cannot pay for editorial ranking or positivity.
9. Conversion: useful free information comes first, optional newsletter invitation follows.
10. Operations: full production/expiry/rollback approvals are separate; one-week low-cost measurement matters more than repeated feature-building.
