# SK8 Scoop daily supervised prospect outreach

Status: active operating runbook.

## Purpose

Create a small, high-quality prospect batch on business days without turning SK8 Scoop into an unsupervised cold-email system.

The standing cadence is up to five prospects per weekday. A smaller batch, including zero, is correct when there are not enough genuinely eligible prospects.

## Daily sequence

1. Identify current commercial triggers relevant to active SK8 Scoop opportunities.
2. Verify the trigger from a current primary or reliable source.
3. Check the main advertising CRM for duplicates, previous contact, replies, objections and suppression.
4. Check Gmail/outreach records for recent contact that may not yet be reflected in the CRM.
5. Classify the intended recipient/contact route as corporate, individual or unknown under the current advertising compliance rules.
6. Reject or hold any prospect whose contact basis, operator identity, trigger or fit is unclear.
7. Draft a short personalised first-contact email for each eligible prospect.
8. Present the batch to Paul for approval.
9. Send only the specifically approved messages.
10. Log the send and next eligible follow-up date.
11. Stop immediately on reply, decline, objection, opt-out, stale trigger or any reason further contact would damage trust.

## Daily batch size

Maximum: 5 first-contact prospects per weekday.

This is a quality ceiling, not a quota. Do not fill the batch with weak prospects to reach five.

## Approval gate

Preparing a batch is automatic. Sending is not.

No prospect email is sent until Paul explicitly approves the batch or named messages. Approval of one day's batch does not authorise future batches.

## Contact rules

- Prefer verified corporate-business routes.
- Do not cold-email individual or unknown recipients unless the current compliance rules clearly allow it.
- Never guess an email address.
- Never send to a held or suppressed prospect.
- Do not use purchased bulk email lists.
- Include appropriate opt-out wording where required.
- Do not imply an existing relationship that does not exist.

## Message standard

Each first contact should normally include:

- one current, verified business-specific hook;
- why it is locally relevant to SK8 readers;
- one concrete advertising angle;
- the current approved TEST/GROW route only where genuinely suitable;
- one low-friction CTA;
- Paul's name and SK8 Scoop;
- appropriate opt-out wording.

Do not dump a media pack, fabricate scarcity, quote unverified audience figures or promise results.

## Follow-up

Default first follow-up is about seven days after first contact.

A follow-up is eligible only if, on that day:

- the trigger remains current;
- no reply/decline/opt-out has been received;
- CRM and suppression checks still pass;
- the contact route remains appropriate;
- the follow-up adds a useful specific thought rather than repeating the first message.

Do not mechanically run a multi-email sequence.

## Sending channel

Approved outreach may be sent through the connected SK8 Scoop email tooling. The sending identity must use a verified SK8 Scoop-controlled domain and replies must route to `contact@sk8scoop.com`.

## Logging

For every sent prospect email record:

- business;
- recipient/contact route;
- trigger and source;
- date sent;
- campaign/opportunity;
- subject;
- status;
- next eligible follow-up date;
- reply/opt-out/suppression outcome.

The main advertising CRM remains the source of truth for prospecting and suppression.

## Relationship to inbound advertising

The daily prospect system is separate from the public advertiser enquiry flow. A prospect who replies positively still goes through the normal suitability, scope, approval and payment process. No outreach message creates an entitlement to advertising or editorial coverage.
