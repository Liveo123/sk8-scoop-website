## Evergreen trigger queue

These do not wait for a season:

- new opening
- new service
- new class/course
- new event
- bookable workshop/experience
- limited-time useful offer
- expansion or new local location
- recruitment only where advertising inventory/product is explicitly approved for it

For every trigger, record the observed source/date and re-check it immediately before outreach.

## Weekly operating output

Every Monday the commercial system should eventually produce only actionable items:

1. opportunities entering acquisition window;
2. high-fit existing advertisers due for reactivation;
3. high-score new prospects not yet contacted;
4. campaigns approaching booking/creative deadline;
5. inventory that is genuinely limited/full;
6. finished campaigns awaiting outcome feedback;
7. stale opportunities to close rather than endlessly chase.

Avoid a calendar that merely reports dates. Its job is to create a short next-action queue.
