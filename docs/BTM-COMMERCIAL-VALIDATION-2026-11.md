## Marketing proposition

**Who:** Parent/guardian planning a birthday for ages 8–11, with an accessible computer/tablet and willingness to host a team puzzle activity.

**Outcome:** Around 90–130 minutes of structured, cooperative mystery play, contingent on playtesting; a digital kit usable without Wi-Fi after download.

**Differentiation:** Illustrated progressive offline browser game *plus* printables, host guide and certificate, rather than a static PDF puzzle pack.

**Challenge:** At GBP 14.99, competing printable products at GBP 5–10 make price comparison easy. The sales page needs a genuine 30–60-second non-spoiler sample and a clearer host prep checklist before being made public. Do not suggest no setup if parent preparation is required.

**Primary offer:** Pay GBP 14.99 for one adventure. Keep the three-reader reward pilot as a separate secondary CTA. Do not advertise an unrestricted protected hosted product as an existing paid feature, since only the pilot portal is built.

## Minimum viable validation

**Stage A: family experience** (no selling yet)
- Ask 2–3 independent households with children in the age band to try the *protected portal or local file*.
- Observe setup time, completion time, maximum assistance, favourite/least favourite case, whether printable pages are clear and whether an adult could run it without the creator.
- Essential pass: no blocking puzzle bugs, no unsolvable paths, most children remain engaged, adult can run it without extensive help. Record unscripted complaints. Reject a claim such as "everyone loved it" without evidence.

**Stage B: checkout and delivery** (owner permission required before changes)
- Replace the v8 test ZIP with final release candidate only after family feedback.
- Payhip Invisible → temporary Unlisted for a supervised 100% coupon checkout. Verify receipt email, ZIP access, virus/malware false positives, Windows file opening, browser/PDF print quality. Return to Invisible after trial, noting visibility also affects older buyers' access.
- A real bank-card payment requires explicit approval; do not run one automatically.
- Update product age/group/duration and digital-fulfilment description; verify UK digital-content refund/cancellation statements under applicable law.

**Stage C: 30-day demand test** (separately approved)
- First use a small number of suitable organically reached SK8 parents, local parenting contacts and existing voluntarily interested readers; never broadcast to the entire list without consent.
- Track *qualified landing-page views*, paid checkouts, refunds/support requests, buyer feedback and owner labour. Separate customers from referral reward claims.
- At 100 qualified visits, >=3 purchases with few support requests is an encouraging early signal, 1–2 is inconclusive and 0 warrants message/targeting rework, not immediate price slashing. This small sample is not statistical proof.
- Stop or simplify if the owner spends more than a few hours/week supporting sales without a credible route to repeat conversions.

## Three criticism-and-fix cycles

1. **Commercial positioning:** Replaced generic "not another worksheet" hero with adult buyer job-to-be-done; retained price while showing reasons it differs from a static PDF.
2. **Truth/friction:** Clarified optional printing, adult host role, download/extraction, approximate runtime as provisional; did not promise hosted post-purchase access or instant effortless setup.
3. **Trust and release:** No checkout activation, no publication, no newsletter send, no unverified testimonials, no automatic reward claim or payment-test charge.
