# Issue 5 combined performance correction

The website originally showed only the Issue 5 main-send rates. The resend was sent to 174 people who had not opened the first send and therefore added further unique activity.

## MailerLite figures supplied

- Main send: 256 recipients, 37.89% opened, 3.52% clicked.
- Resend to non-openers: 174 recipients, 20.69% opened, 1.15% clicked.

The displayed percentages correspond to approximately:

- Main send: approximately 97 recorded opens and 9 clicks.
- Resend: approximately 36 recorded opens and 2 additional clicks.

Calculated against the original 256-recipient campaign audience, the combined observed result is approximately:

- **133 recorded opens / 256 = 51.95%, displayed as 52.0% effective open reach including resend**
- **11 clicks / 256 = 4.3% combined click reach**

The percentages from the two sends must not simply be added or averaged because they use different denominators. The resend was sent to people classed as non-openers at that point, but later opens can create slight overlap. These figures are rounded from the MailerLite reports and should be treated as dated Issue 5 evidence, not a perfect unique-person count, long-term average or guarantee.
