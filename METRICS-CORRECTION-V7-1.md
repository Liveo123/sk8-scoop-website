# Issue 5 combined performance correction

The website originally showed only the Issue 5 main-send rates. The resend was sent to 174 people who had not opened the first send and therefore added further unique activity.

## MailerLite figures supplied

- Main send: 256 recipients, 35.16% opened, 3.52% clicked.
- Resend to non-openers: 174 recipients, 16.67% opened, 1.15% clicked.

The displayed percentages correspond to approximately:

- Main send: 90 opens and 9 clicks.
- Resend: 29 additional opens and 2 additional clicks.

Calculated against the original 256-recipient campaign audience, the combined observed result is approximately:

- **119 opens / 256 = 46.5% combined open reach**
- **11 clicks / 256 = 4.3% combined click reach**

The percentages from the two sends must not simply be added because they use different denominators. These figures are rounded from the MailerLite reports and should be treated as dated Issue 5 evidence, not a long-term average or a guarantee.
