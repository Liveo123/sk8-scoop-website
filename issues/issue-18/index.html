GO · THIS WEEKEND

The final steam weekend at Anson Engine Museum

24–25 October · 10am–4pm · Anson Road, Poynton SK12 1TD

The museum closes its 2026 public steam season with running engines and demonstrations of traditional crafts. A practical outing for anyone keen on machinery and local engineering history.

Event-day prices: £10 adults; £2.50 ages 13–16; free for under-13s. Individual engines and demonstrations can change, so confirm before travelling.

BOOK AHEAD · THURSDAY 5 NOVEMBER

Woodford Bonfire: tickets, times and the parking catch

Woodford Community Centre · Gates from 5.45pm

The children’s firework display is at 7pm and the main display at 8pm; the event closes at 9pm. Advance tickets are required. Check the live booking page for prices and availability.

There is no parking at the community centre. The organisers direct drivers to Notcutts Garden Centre, subject to capacity, and encourage walking or public transport where possible.

DISCOVER · LOCAL HISTORY

Pat Phoenix and the boxing kangaroo

REDA’S · 225–229 FINNEY LANE, HEALD GREEN

By 1947 the former bank premises had become Reda’s, short for Radio and Electrical Domestic Appliances. Residents remember actor Pat Phoenix attending a 1963 reopening and a kangaroo wearing boxing gloves.

One former resident recalls the date as Saturday 18 May 1963; another remembers the kangaroo being called “Redaroo”. Those details are recollections, not independent proof that an actual fight took place. The older photographs on the heritage site are not automatically licensed for republication here.

PLAN · AVOID THE HASSLE

Paths, roads and a proper woodland walk

GATLEY · FROM 26 OCTOBER

Footpaths 48 and 51 close with no official diversion

Stockport Council says Footpath 48 between Yew Tree Grove and Grasmere Road, and Footpath 51 between Motcombe Grove and Footpath 48, will close for housing-development works from Monday 26 October.

The order allows up to six months but works could end sooner. No official diversion is provided. Check the council notice rather than improvising directions.

QUARRY BANK · STYAL

Follow the National Trust’s Southern Woods walk

Choose a one-mile short route or a 1.5-mile full walk starting from Quarry Bank’s Mill Yard. Allow about 45 minutes and follow the purple waymarkers. This is a genuine published route, not a line drawn from guesswork.

There are slopes; the full trail is not fully wheelchair- or pushchair-accessible. Dogs are welcome on leads. The walking route is separate from the mill’s ticketed admissions.
