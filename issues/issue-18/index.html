Anson steam weekend, a boxing kangaroo and Gatley path closures

The useful, the local and the mildly nosy: days out, half-term help and changes that matter across SK8.

Anson Engine Museum’s final steam weekend

Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October, 10am–4pm, Anson Road, Poynton. The museum’s final public steam weekend of its 2026 season includes engines and craft demonstrations. Adults £10, ages 13–16 £2.50, under-13s free. Check what is operating before travelling.

Reda’s shop and its boxing kangaroo

At 225–229 Finney Lane, Heald Green, an electrical shop attracted a very unusual guest for its 1963 relaunch. Pat Phoenix and a boxing-gloved kangaroo appear in residents’ memories. Details such as a naming contest are recollections, rather than independently verified records.

Two Gatley footpaths due to close from Monday

From 26 October, Stockport Council plans to close Footpath 48 between Yew Tree Grove and Grasmere Road, and Footpath 51 between Motcombe Grove and Footpath 48, for housing development work. The legal notice permits up to six months. No official diversion is provided, so check the council’s map rather than assuming an alternative route.

Half-term ideas

Nixon’s Farm’s separate Halloween play special is listed for 31 October at £5 per child for a 90-minute supervised session. The SK8 Holiday Club Finder and Halloween guide can help with activities, dates, ages and prices; confirm places directly with organisers.

A mapped woodland walk at Quarry Bank

The National Trust’s Southern Woods walk offers about 1 or 1.5 miles of woodland paths, with purple waymarkers and a granite Ice Age boulder. The full route has slopes and is not accessible throughout for wheelchairs or pushchairs; check the official directions and property access guidance.

Plan ahead

Clocks go back on Sunday 25 October: at 2am BST the time returns to 1am GMT. For local plans, check current Cheadle and Heald Green resurfacing notices and advance tickets for the Woodford Bonfire on 5 November.
