Cheadle's last coding club, Bramall Hall's Christmas and a riverside mystery

Website edition: these articles and event leads are adapted from the Issue 22 draft. This page is not a claim that the Issue 22 email has been sent. Check event organisers for any change in availability.

Go: Bramall Hall's Christmas

Bramall Hall’s roof restoration is complete, just in time for festive floral decorations. For 21–22 November, Hall admission is £7 adults, £5.50 concessions and free for under-16s; the separately booked Elf Trail costs extra.

Read the restoration and admission details.

Discover: two local mysteries

The lost buildings beside Stockport's rivers explores an unexplained oversized doorway and the changing industrial landscape beside the rivers. The doorway’s location and purpose remain unresolved.

The pub at the end of Gatley's tram line takes you to a c.1905 archive postcard of an electric tram beside the Horse & Farrier.

Plan: Cheadle CoderDojo's last session

After twelve years, Cheadle’s volunteer-led coding club is closing. Its final free session is 21 November, 11am–2pm, with advance booking. Read the goodbye and visit details.

Weekend picks, in one place

Saturday 21 November: Woodbank Winter Gathering (free entry; optional £25 workshop), The Light's CBeebies Treasure Island panto, the final matinee of Rope at Players Theatre, an Oasis tribute in Cheadle Hulme, The Underground Revue in Stockport, and the Warehouse Project at Depot Mayfield. Sunday 22 November: Stockport Vinyl Fair (£1 cash entry) and Bramall Hall's final advertised weekend displays and Elf Trail.
