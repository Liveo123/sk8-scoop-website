Important correction

Friday's coverage confused two Heald Green farms. The cattle once crossing Simonsway came from Chamber Hall Farm, not Daisy Bank Farm, which stands on Finney Lane. We apologise and thank Heald Green Heritage for correcting the record. Read the correction and historical context.
