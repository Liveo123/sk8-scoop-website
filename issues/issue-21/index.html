Correction: which farm's cows crossed Simonsway?

We confused two Heald Green farms in Friday's edition. The cattle that crossed Simonsway twice a day belonged to Chamber Hall Farm, not Daisy Bank Farm on Finney Lane. We apologised to Kathy at Heald Green Heritage in the 14 November resend and corrected this website account.
