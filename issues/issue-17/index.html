GO · FREE FAMILY EVENT · SATURDAY 24 OCTOBER

Merseyday of the Dead: two parts, one colourful Saturday

Free entrySat 24 OctoberStockport town centre

12–3pm, Merseyway (Skullstock): a free spooky trail, crafts, sugar-skull glitter tattoos and performances by the Three Amigos.

5–7pm, Redrock (Deadrock): trick-or-treating, Mexican folk dancing, slime, games and a Day of the Dead parade finale.

Costumes are encouraged. Travel, food and optional purchases cost extra. Activities may reach capacity, so check the latest organiser information before travelling.

The Premier Café's remarkable upstairs rooms

THEATRE · 21–24 OCTOBER

CHESS at Stockport Plaza

Stockport Operatic Society stages the Cold War musical with songs by Benny Andersson and Björn Ulvaeus and lyrics by Tim Rice. The reported run is Wednesday 21 to Saturday 24 October, with evening shows and a Saturday matinee. Ticket availability and prices must be confirmed on the theatre's own page.

Three more Halloween outings

  • Bramall Hall Witch's Potion Trail: 22–25 and 27 October–1 November, with £3 per participating child plus standard accompanying adult admission; check Bramall Hall's official page.
  • Halloween Foodie Friday: Friday 30 October, 6–9pm, Stockport Market; free to enter, purchases extra. Check the programme.
  • Cheadle Soul Club Halloween Special: Saturday 31 October at Cheadle and Gatley Conservative Club, £5 on the door, with dress and late-entry rules. Check the organiser listing.

Watch the roads

Issue 17 flagged expected resurfacing on Outwood Road in Heald Green and High Grove Road in Cheadle from 26 October, weather permitting, plus Manchester Road footway changes. Timings and closures may move. Stockport resurfacing updates and Manchester Road works notice are the reliable next steps.
