Heald Green Christmas markets, Gatley’s railway history and indoor play

A free Heald Green market, a closer look at Gatley station and a guide to what indoor play really costs. This is a web companion to the sent edition.

Go: Heald Green Christmas Markets

On Sunday 29 November, the car park behind 232–234 Finney Lane hosts a free market from 11am to 4pm, with stalls, entertainment and the village lights switch-on. The council listing confirms the market hours but does not give a separate switch-on time.

See source and directions at Stockport Council.

Discover: a closer look at Gatley station

It’s easy to hurry through Gatley station and miss the original station building above the Platform 1 ramp. The two platforms are linked by a subway, and the photographed Platform 2 has a shelter rather than a matching station building. Next time you’re passing through, look for the wooden supports under the platform building.

See the station photographs and notes.
