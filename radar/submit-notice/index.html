A few useful things to know

Is it free?

Submitting a notice is always free. Eligible placements are free during the first calendar month after this Noticeboard is published in the Radar newsletter. After that, only charity notices remain free. Other placements require an agreed payment before publication. All notices are editorially reviewed.

What should I send?

A direct link is ideal. If you don't have one, include what, where, when, price or free entry, and how someone can join or book.

Will it be printed?

Not automatically. Radar checks submissions and chooses those useful and relevant to readers. We may edit wording for clarity.

What about my email?

It's for moderation and queries, not publication or automatic newsletter signup. Please only share contact information you have permission to share.
