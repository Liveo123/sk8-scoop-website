A few useful things

What can I submit?

Local clubs, classes, events, fundraisers, volunteer opportunities and useful community notices. If the details are already online, start with the link.

Do I need a finished advert?

No. A link or a few clear details will do. We review submissions and write suitable notices. Any paid placement must be agreed separately.

What happens after I send it?

The editors check the details and may contact you with questions or edited wording. Suitable notices may appear in a future Edit, depending on timing and space.
