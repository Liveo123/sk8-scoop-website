Got something happening locally?

A class, gig, workshop, community event or a worthwhile local offer? We're collecting short notices for South Manchester Radar, a local things-to-do newsletter.

We'll do the writing

You don't need to write a polished announcement. Send your existing web page, ticket link, poster details or a few notes and we can turn them into a short notice for you to check.

  1. Tell us what it is and where/when it's happening.
  2. Share the price (or free) and a page where details can be checked.
  3. We draft a short notice and check the facts with you before any publication.

Current pilot terms: submitting a notice is free for at least the first month. Future optional business notices are proposed at £10 per month, with no automatic payment or commitment. Community and charity notices can remain free. All notices are subject to editorial review and space; sending information does not guarantee publication.

Submission inbox and form: being prepared. This preview is not yet accepting submissions. For now, invited organisations can reply directly to the Radar outreach email.

What's suitable?

Local classes, performances, creative workshops, meet-ups, community activities, relevant launches and genuinely useful local offers. Radar remains a things-to-do publication, not a general news or classifieds service.

Please don't submit sensitive personal information. Photographs or logos will only be used with appropriate permission. Published notices are fact-checked and may be edited for length and clarity; paid notices will be labelled.
