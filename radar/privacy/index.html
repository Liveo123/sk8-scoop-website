THE SMALL PRINT

Radar privacy notice.

South Manchester Radar is an independent JiveLoop Collective newsletter. We use your email address to send the free newsletter you request. It is separate from SK8 Scoop: joining one does not automatically join the other.

What we collect

When you join Radar, we collect your email address and your affirmative sign-up choice. The signup system may also process the date, time, IP address and anti-spam verification information. We use Cloudflare Turnstile to reduce abusive or automated sign-ups. Our subscriber list is held in MailerLite.

Why we use it

We use your email address to deliver Radar and manage newsletter preferences, based on your consent. Technical information is used to protect the sign-up service and prevent abuse. We do not automatically add Radar subscribers to SK8 Scoop, or vice versa.

Optional one-referral reward

If you choose to take part in the Radar referral programme, we use an opaque personal code, MailerLite subscriber identifiers, the date you entered a code and an optional, separate email-confirmation link to check that one genuinely new reader independently joined Radar. We record an earned reward once only and keep a minimal private audit log. The separate referral confirmation does not change the newsletter's single-opt-in process.

No reader supplies us with another person's email address to use the programme, and we do not send marketing to a referrer's contacts. We use an existing transactional email service to send requested code and confirmation notices. If a code was entered in error, or you want referral records removed, contact us. Records are subject to reasonable retention and deletion requests.

Unsubscribe and your rights

You can unsubscribe using the link in a Radar email. Because Radar and SK8 Scoop currently use the same MailerLite account, the general unsubscribe control may stop all emails from that account. If you only want to leave one publication, email contact@sk8scoop.com with your preference. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, subject to applicable law.

Who processes information

We use MailerLite to manage subscriptions and send email, and Cloudflare to host the signup service and verify sign-up requests. Information may be handled outside the UK subject to the relevant suppliers' safeguards. No advertising trackers are loaded on this signup page.
