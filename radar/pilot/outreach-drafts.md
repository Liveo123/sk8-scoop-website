# Radar noticeboard: 25 research-based draft openings

STATUS: UNSENT. Contact eligibility, recipient suppression and current event facts must be checked before sending. The £10/month future option requires express acceptance, never automatic billing. For charities keep free notices separate. Do not promise publication.

## Email template (personalised first line, 65-80 words)

**Subject:** Could we include [organisation]'s [activity]?

Hi,

I spotted your [specific activity] and thought it would suit South Manchester Radar, our new local things-to-do newsletter.

We're inviting a few local organisers to share short community notices. It's free for at least the next month. After that, optional business notices will be £10/month, only if you choose to continue.

**Could you reply with a link to your next event?** We'll write the notice and send it for your approval.

Paul
South Manchester Radar

If you'd rather not hear from us again, just reply 'no thanks'.
