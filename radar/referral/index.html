A little thank-you for Radar readers

One new Radar reader. Four adventures for you.

Radar subscribers can get a personal referral code. When a genuinely new reader independently enters your code while joining Radar and confirms their email address, you qualify for our four-adventure South Manchester Adventure Atlas.

The guide: Creepy Carnival, Mission: Mad Hatter, a velodrome flying lap and Mersey kayaking (seasonal). The digital reward includes the web guide and a downloadable PDF. Activities themselves may cost money.

You never need to give us anybody else's email address. The referral programme is optional, and the ordinary Radar newsletter remains free.

For privacy, we'll always show the same confirmation, whether or not an address is subscribed. If you're on the list, check your inbox. A code request may take up to twelve hours before it can be repeated.
