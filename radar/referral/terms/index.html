Radar referral reward terms

Promoter: South Manchester Radar, a JiveLoop Collective publication. Contact contact@sk8scoop.com, 136 Stockport Road, Cheadle Heath, Stockport SK3 0JE.

The reward

One eligible new South Manchester Radar reader who voluntarily enters an existing subscriber’s valid referral code while joining Radar and independently confirms their email address qualifies the existing subscriber to receive one digital copy of The Unordinary Four, a four-adventure guide published as the South Manchester Adventure Atlas. The reward includes the web guide and downloadable PDF. The Radar newsletter and guide are free; booking or taking part in the activities may have a charge.

Who qualifies

The referrer must be a current, active Radar subscriber with an issued code. The referred reader must be a genuinely new Radar subscriber, voluntarily opt in to Radar, enter the code at initial signup and use the separate email-confirmation link within 48 hours. Existing Radar subscribers, duplicate and self-referrals, fake and abusive signups, and unconfirmed addresses do not qualify. A person can earn the digital reward once in the pilot.

Timing and fairness

The offer is open-ended and has no fixed closing date. We may amend or withdraw it prospectively and will update this page if we do. A qualifying reader will be notified and sent the reward within seven days after the referred reader’s confirmation, subject to successful eligibility checks and a working email and guide destination. We will honour any referral that qualified while the offer was open. Contact us if you believe a credit was missed. A reward link may be shared by its recipient and is not DRM-protected.

Privacy

We process subscriber IDs, an opaque referral code, limited signup and confirmation timestamps and a reward status for referral validation, without storing friends' email addresses in a referral ledger. No reader is asked to supply another person's details or send promotional emails. See our privacy notice for subscription processing, rights and contact details.
