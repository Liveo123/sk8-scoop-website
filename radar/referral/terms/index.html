The reward

One eligible, new South Manchester Radar reader who voluntarily enters an existing subscriber's valid referral code while joining Radar, and confirms ownership of their email, qualifies the existing subscriber for one copy of the digital South Manchester Adventure Atlas featuring four outings. The digital reward comprises a web guide and downloadable PDF. The newsletter and guide are free; booking or taking part in the activities may have a charge.

Who qualifies

The referrer must be a current, active Radar subscriber with an issued code. The referred reader must be a genuinely new Radar subscriber, voluntarily opt in to Radar, enter the code at initial signup and use the separate email-confirmation link within 48 hours. Existing Radar subscribers, duplicate and self-referrals, fake and abusive signups, and unconfirmed addresses do not qualify. A person can earn the digital reward once in the pilot.

Timing and fairness

The pilot's opening and closing dates will be clearly displayed once approved, before the programme becomes active. A qualifying reader will be notified within seven days after approval, subject to a working email and guide destination. We reserve the right to investigate inaccurate claims fairly; contact us if you believe a credit was missed. A reward link may be shared by its recipient and is not DRM-protected.

Privacy

We process subscriber IDs, an opaque referral code, limited signup and confirmation timestamps and a reward status for referral validation, without storing friends' email addresses in a referral ledger. No reader is asked to supply another person's details or send promotional emails. See our privacy notice for subscription processing, rights and contact details.
