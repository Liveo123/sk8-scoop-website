How it works

Organisers can send a link, poster or a few details. Radar drafts a concise notice, checks the facts and sends the wording back for approval. The editor decides whether it belongs here.

Notices are free for at least the first month of the live pilot. Any later £10/month business listing is optional. Charity/community notices can remain free. Commercial notices are clearly labelled and never control independent editorial choices.
