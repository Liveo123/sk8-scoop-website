THE PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before you join

Is Radar free, and how often will I hear from you?

Yes. Radar is a free weekly newsletter. You'll receive a welcome email, a short follow-up, and then the regular weekly editions. We won't pretend every issue will suit every reader.

Which areas does it cover?

Chorlton, Didsbury, West Didsbury, Withington and Burnage come first. Occasionally we'll go further across South Manchester or into the city centre if an experience is worth the trip.

Is this the same as SK8 Scoop?

No. Radar helps you discover things to do, especially at weekends. SK8 Scoop is a separate publication covering local life around Cheadle, Cheadle Hulme, Gatley and Heald Green. Signing up here adds you to Radar only.

Can I leave Radar without losing SK8 Scoop?

You can ask us to remove you from Radar only by emailing contact@sk8scoop.com. Because both publications currently share a MailerLite account, a standard email unsubscribe link may stop emails from both. We're working towards clearer self-service choices.

Are recommendations paid for by advertisers?

Editorial recommendations are selected for reader usefulness. Any sponsored placement will be clearly labelled, and payment does not guarantee inclusion in the editorial shortlist.
