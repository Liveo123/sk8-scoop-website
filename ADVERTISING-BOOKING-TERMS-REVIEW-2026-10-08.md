# SK8 Scoop Advertising Booking Terms — REVIEW DRAFT (not yet part of a live contract)
8 October 2026. These terms are to be reviewed with the booking process in PR #183 and legal identity/tax status before publication. They do not change any existing agreement.

## Before a booking
An enquiry is not a booking or reservation. SK8 Scoop confirms the business, proposed advert, publication surface, relevant Guide and section, position, publication/start/end dates, agreed campaign objective and linked destination, price including the applicable tax treatment, and any special restrictions in writing. The customer must confirm these details before a payment request is issued. A booking is accepted only after SK8 Scoop confirms it and the agreed payment terms are satisfied.

## Standard one-off options
- Newsletter advert (£35): one clearly identified paid advert in one agreed newsletter issue.
- Guide card (£28): one paid, clearly identified card in one agreed SK8 Scoop Guide. A year-round Guide card runs for 30 days from the agreed start; seasonal placements cover the separately agreed remaining promotional period.
- Guide + newsletter (£60): one Guide card and one newsletter advert, each according to the agreed dates.
- Guide section sponsor (£96): a visibly labelled, more prominent position in one agreed Guide section. Exact inventory, deliverables and period must be confirmed before booking.
- Main Guide sponsor (£125): a visibly labelled lead commercial sponsorship position in an agreed Guide. Exact inventory, deliverables and period must be confirmed before booking.

All products are subject to available, relevant inventory. Displayed prices are not promises of a particular date or Guide position. There is no automatic renewal. Do not imply category exclusivity unless the individually agreed booking expressly states its limited scope and period; exclusivity never applies to independent editorial content.

## Advert preparation and approval
The advertiser supplies accurate offers, restrictions, contact/booking destinations and material facts, and confirms rights to their logo, photographs or creative. SK8 Scoop may produce an advert using these facts or use supplied suitable artwork. The customer sees the final copy/design before publication and should identify mistakes promptly. Material changes after approval, including changes to the agreed offer or artwork, are subject to production feasibility and separate written agreement where extra cost is involved.

## Standards and independent editorial
All paid material will be clearly labelled. We may decline unsuitable, inaccurate, unlawful or misleading promotions; a payment never entitles an advertiser to favourable, preferential or exclusive editorial coverage. Neither sponsored positions nor the existence of a booking establish independently verified quality or a recommendation.

## Payment, cancellation and delivery failures
The agreed total and applicable tax treatment will be clearly stated before payment. Payment is normally required before publication, unless otherwise agreed in writing. A requested cancellation before publication will be assessed fairly against the timing, any individually agreed terms and demonstrable committed work; no undisclosed cancellation penalty is imposed. If SK8 Scoop fails to deliver an agreed paid placement, we will offer a suitable agreed replacement date or an appropriate refund for the undelivered part. A materially defective placement will be assessed and remedied fairly. Nothing here restricts rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## Results and reporting
SK8 Scoop will provide a concise summary of available tracked advertisement-link clicks following the agreed period where measurement is available. Tracked clicks are not enquiries, bookings, customers or verified sales. Tracking limitations, consent requirements, browser blocking or unavailable data may prevent complete measurement; missing evidence must never be reported as zero without justification. No minimum response or commercial outcome is guaranteed.

## Contact and dispute handling
Report a concern promptly by replying to the booking correspondence or emailing contact@sk8scoop.com, quoting the agreed campaign. We will consider documented evidence, explain practical remediation and aim to resolve concerns fairly. These terms are governed by applicable law and cannot override non-excludable statutory rights.
