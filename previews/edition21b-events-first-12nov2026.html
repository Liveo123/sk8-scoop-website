Best of this weekend

Saturday: three picks, not a wall of choices

Saturday 14 November · Bramhall

Bramhall festive market

Festive shopping in Bramhall, with independent traders and seasonal browsing close to SK8. A practical choice if you want an outing without a long journey. Check stall details, opening times and any activities before leaving; purchases are extra.

Saturday 14 November · Stockroom, Stockport

SUBTEXT: Doodle ’n Splice filmmaking workshop

Free creative opportunity for 11–25-year-olds. A drop-in runs noon–12.45pm followed by the main hand-drawn film workshop from 1–3pm. Participants use personal objects and archival inspiration to create animation. Check places and accessibility directly with Stockroom.

Saturday 14 November · Tatton Park, Knutsford

Yuletide at Tatton Park

An illuminated outdoor folklore trail with live characters, music, puppetry and theatrical moments. Timed entry runs 3–7.30pm and the organiser suggests allowing about 90 minutes for the approximately 1km route. Source Issue 21 v25 listed adult £19.50 and child £11.50 tickets for 14 November, subject to recheck; consider the total family cost before booking.

Sunday highlight

Stockport's big free afternoon

Sunday 15 November · Stockport town centre

Stockport Spectacular Sunday

A free town-centre programme of street performances with the Christmas lights finale. Previous research reported more than 50 shows, over 200 performers and a 4.45pm switch-on. Treat those programme figures and the exact finale time as details to reconfirm with the council before sending. Expect busy streets and allow extra time to move between performance spots.

A local youth-theatre production that avoids a journey into Manchester. A useful option for families looking for an evening out. The source editorial master lists performances across 15–21 November; times, ticket prices and availability need a fresh check on Brookdale's booking page.

Monday 16–Saturday 21 November · Anfield Road, Cheadle Hulme

Rope at Players Theatre

Patrick Hamilton's tightly wound thriller follows two young men who conceal a murder and then entertain guests while the evidence sits close by. It is a distinctive, genuinely local theatre choice. Check individual performance times, prices and age guidance with the company before booking.

Manchester Art Fair

An indoor browse through contemporary art, exhibitors and talks. The Issue 21 v25 research listed more than 180 exhibitors, a separate Thursday preview and Friday–Sunday general tickets from £15 plus booking fee; check the current programme and any under-16 conditions. Better for an afternoon of exploring than a quick dash between appointments.

Book ahead

Four plans for 20–22 November

Friday 20–Sunday 22 November · Rochdale Town Hall Square and gardens

Rochdale Ignite Fire Festival

A free evening festival of fire, light, sound and performance, planned for 5–8pm each evening. The BODY installation is organised into six atmospheric zones; the Christmas lights switch-on was listed for Sunday at around 5.30pm. Expect smoke, flashing effects and crowds, so check sensory suitability and the latest programme before travelling.

Woodbank Winter Gathering

A low-cost community outing with gifts, seasonal activities and refreshments, beginning at 11am. Entry was listed as free, with wreath-making workshops costing £25 and requiring separate booking. Saturday 21–Sunday 22 November · Bowlers Exhibition Centre, Trafford Park

Manchester Coffee Festival

A two-day festival of coffee tastings, talks, competitions and music. Public entry is on Saturday and Sunday, not the Friday trade day. Session ticket prices vary; organiser guidance listed £10 daily parking and free under-12 admission with the appropriate child ticket and paying adult. Good for serious coffee fans, less useful if you only want one cappuccino.
