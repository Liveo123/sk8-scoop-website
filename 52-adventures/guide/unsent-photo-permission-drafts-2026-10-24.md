## Clearance recording template

For every affirmative reply record: date, sender identity, exact filenames and URLs covered, what uses were expressly authorised (website/email/social/commercial advertising-supported context), photographer/name, required credit, cropping/modification restrictions, duration/withdrawal terms and original reply location. Only then mark **PERMISSION** in the central Image Rights Register. A request sent or unacknowledged is still **not** permission.

Where the exact photo is unavailable, the unpublished guide retains its already licensed image; for Underbanks, Hat Works, Transport, Science & Industry and Goyt Valley, there are five separately licensed Commons alternatives available for Paul's comparison.
