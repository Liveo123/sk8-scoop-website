The principle

Do not buy a dead-end advert.

A reader who is interested should have somewhere relevant to go next. That might be your booking page, a useful SK8 Scoop continuation, a guide, an offer page or another clear action.

Next Useful Experience

One useful thing should lead naturally to the next.

We call this our Next Useful Experience approach. Instead of dropping an interested reader onto a generic sales message, the campaign gives them a relevant next step that helps them decide or act.

Newsletter

Earn attention with a concise, useful commercial message.

Website or guide

Continue with details, context, an offer, booking information or another genuinely useful page.

Business outcome

Agree the action that matters: enquiry, booking, quote request, redemption, purchase, registration or another measurable result.

What gets measured

Clicks are evidence, not the whole result.

Before the campaign, agree the main action that would make the advert useful to your business. Afterwards, use the available evidence to decide whether to keep it, change it or stop.

Depending on the campaign, useful measures can include:

tracked advert-link clicks, booking-page visits, enquiries, quote requests, offer redemptions, purchases, registrations and any business outcomes you choose to report back.
