Questions

What local advertisers usually need to know.

Do I need finished artwork or advert copy?

No. Send the useful facts, a suitable link and the reader action you want. SK8 Scoop prepares the placement for approval.

Can I buy editorial coverage?

No. Paid placements are labelled and kept separate from editorial judgement, ranking and recommendation.

Which option should I choose?

You do not need to choose. Use the fit check and we will recommend the smallest sensible route.

Will the campaign renew automatically?

No. Standard campaigns do not automatically renew. Review the evidence before deciding what happens next.
