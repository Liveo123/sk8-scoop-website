What gets measured

Clicks matter. Business outcomes matter more.

Before the campaign, agree the action that would make it useful to the business. Afterwards, keep SK8-measured response separate from outcomes the business reports back.

Questions before spending anything

What local advertisers usually need to know.

Do I need finished artwork or advert copy?

No. Send the useful facts, any suitable image or link you have, and the reader action you want. SK8 Scoop prepares the reader-facing placement for approval.

Can I buy editorial coverage?

No. Paid placements are labelled and kept separate from editorial judgement, ranking and recommendation.

When should I use GROW instead of TEST?

Only when the Free & Cheap Guide is genuinely relevant to the offer and gives the reader useful extra context. A bigger package is not automatically a better one.

Can I advertise on SK8Scoop.com?

Yes, subject to page fit and availability. WEBSITE placements are clearly labelled, designed to sit naturally within the site, and priced according to the selected placement and duration.

Will the campaign renew automatically?

No. TEST and GROW do not automatically renew. Website duration is agreed in advance. The point is to review the evidence before deciding what happens next.

Can I buy exclusivity or a sponsorship?

Not as standard inventory. If a genuinely useful sponsorship idea exists, it can be discussed separately rather than implied as an off-the-shelf product.
