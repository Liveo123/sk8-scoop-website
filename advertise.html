Questions before spending anything

What local advertisers usually need to know.

Do I need finished artwork or advert copy?

No. Send the useful facts, any suitable image or link you have, and the reader action you want. SK8 Scoop prepares the reader-facing placement for approval.

Can I buy editorial coverage?

No. Paid placements are labelled and kept separate from editorial judgement, ranking and recommendation.

What does the £35 newsletter advert include?

One clearly labelled sponsored placement in an agreed weekly newsletter issue, with preparation and a summary of measured advert-link response.

How long does a Guide card stay visible?

A seasonal card stays in the agreed Guide for the dates stated in your booking confirmation. We give you the exact start and end dates before payment. Year-round Guide advertising, where offered, is quoted separately.

How do I pay?

After we confirm suitability, availability, price and dates, you approve the advert proof and receive a secure Stripe invoice or payment link. This enquiry form does not take payment.

Will the campaign renew automatically?

No. The introductory first-campaign rate does not automatically renew. The point is to review the evidence before deciding what happens next.

Can I buy website advertising, exclusivity or a sponsorship?

Recurring website sponsorship and exclusivity are not standard inventory at the moment. For the 2026 Halloween & Half-Term Guide only, the time-limited section and main-guide sponsorships shown above are available subject to fit and availability. They buy labelled visibility, not editorial influence.
