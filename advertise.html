COMMON QUESTIONS

The things a local business should know before enquiring.

No inflated media kit, no invented reach claims and no requirement to arrive with finished advert creative.

Do I need artwork or finished copy?

No. Send the useful facts, the destination link and what you want the reader to do. SK8 Scoop prepares the placement for your approval.

Can I buy editorial coverage?

No. Advertising is labelled and separate from editorial judgement. Payment does not buy favourable coverage, recommendation or ranking.

Should I choose TEST or GROW?

TEST is the default first route. GROW only makes sense when the Free & Cheap Guide is a genuine contextual fit for the same objective.

Is there an automatic renewal?

No. TEST is deliberately a small first campaign. Review the evidence before deciding whether to repeat or change anything.

Can I buy category exclusivity or a long sponsorship?

Not as standard inventory at the moment. Recurring sponsorship, exclusivity and other larger placements remain behind readiness gates rather than being sold simply because they could exist.

What happens after I send the form?

SK8 Scoop checks fit, scope and timing first. If it makes sense, the campaign route and creative are agreed before payment and before anything runs.
