Questions before you book

Advertising FAQs

What exactly do I get for £35, £28 or £60?

£35 buys one sponsored advert in an agreed newsletter issue. £28 buys one card in an available SK8 Scoop Guide. £60 includes one of each. We prepare the wording and presentation, and you approve the draft before it runs.

Which Guides can I advertise in?

You can request any SK8 Scoop Guide in the form, including 52 Adventures, Free & Cheap, Halloween, Christmas and the other Guides. Not every Guide has paid space available all the time. We confirm the suitable Guide and availability before any payment.

How long will my Guide advert stay live?

In year-round Guides, the standard paid card lasts 30 days from the agreed start date. In seasonal Guides, it lasts for the agreed remaining promotional period. We give you the exact start and end dates before you pay. Older archived pages are not counted as guaranteed advertising.

What do £96 and £125 sponsorships include?

£96 is for a more prominent, clearly labelled sponsored position in an agreed Guide section. £125 is for the main Guide sponsorship position, where available. We confirm the placement, dates and any specific inclusions before payment. Neither buys editorial influence or an automatic exclusive right.

Do I need artwork or advertising copy?

No. Send your useful facts and website or booking link. If you have a suitable logo or image, you can share it afterwards. We prepare the advert for your approval.

When and how do I pay?

First send the enquiry. We check fit and space, agree the dates and send you an advert proof. Once you approve, we send a secure Stripe payment or invoice link for the agreed amount. This page does not take payment.

Will I receive a results report?

Yes. After your agreed placement period, we send a short summary of available tracked advert-link clicks, including the newsletter and Guide where applicable. We cannot guarantee clicks or know whether a click became a sale unless you tell us.

Do adverts renew automatically?

No. All the displayed prices are for one-off placements. Any new campaign needs a separate agreement and payment.

Can I choose my publication date or request another format?

Tell us your preferred month and Guide. We'll confirm an available issue date or Guide period before you book. If you need something different, email contact@sk8scoop.com.

Can advertising buy a recommendation or news coverage?

No. Paid placements are clearly labelled and separate from our editorial decisions. See our editorial and advertising policy.
