Measurement

See what actually happened.

We keep what was shown, what was clicked or scanned, and any genuine advertiser-confirmed enquiries, bookings or sales separate. A click is not described as a sale.

Possible measures include:

tracked advert-link clicks, booking-page visits, enquiries, quote requests, offer redemptions, registrations and advertiser-reported downstream outcomes where available.

Paid visibility. Independent editorial.

Advertising can buy clearly labelled exposure. It cannot buy a recommendation, favourable editorial coverage, editorial ranking, a Top Pick badge or protection from criticism.

Campaigns are subject to suitability and availability, and results are not guaranteed.
