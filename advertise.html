After the advert

You won’t be left wondering whether anybody clicked.

The report is intended to be genuinely useful. It separates what was delivered, what readers did and what we can reasonably learn from it. It will not pretend that a click automatically equals a sale.

Where the data exists, your report can include:

issue and placement date, newsletter recipients, available open/delivery context, tracked advert-link clicks, click rate or other meaningful response rate, QR scans or offer use if those were part of the campaign, and any advertiser-reported enquiries or bookings you choose to share.
