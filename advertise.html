What is not standard inventory yet?

Recurring sponsorship, category exclusivity, paid public-website placements and paid subscriber-area placements are still behind readiness gates. They are not being presented here as ordinary products just because they exist in the longer-term plan.

How a campaign works

Your goal → SK8 recommends → you approve → campaign runs → evidence → next decision.

The idea is deliberately small: remove media-planning faff, make the commercial status obvious, and learn from a real reader action.

What gets measured

Clicks are evidence, not the whole result.

Before the campaign, agree the action that would make it useful to the business. Afterwards, separate what SK8 Scoop can measure directly from outcomes the business reports back.

Depending on the campaign, useful evidence can include:

tracked advert-link clicks, booking-page visits, enquiries, quote requests, offer redemptions, purchases, registrations and other agreed outcomes. Results are not guaranteed.
