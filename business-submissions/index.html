How a tip becomes something publishable

The point is not to collect everything. It is to turn useful, checkable local information into something a reader can act on.

01 / LOCALIs it actually SK8-relevant?Specific place, audience or local consequence.
02 / CURRENTIs the timing still useful?Dates, deadlines and availability checked.
03 / VERIFIEDCan the facts be checked?Official source, organiser or responsible contact.
04 / USEFULWhy should a reader care?Clear benefit, change, saving or reason to know.
05 / RESULTPublish, hold or askUseful facts move forward. Weak ones do not.

What gets prioritised

  • Strong SK8 relevance
  • Useful dates, prices and links
  • Free or affordable family value
  • Important practical updates
  • Something genuinely new or distinctive

What usually gets held back

  • Expired or vague information
  • No checkable source
  • Ordinary promotion with no reader value
  • Claims that cannot be supported
