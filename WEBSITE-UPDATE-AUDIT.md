# SK8 Scoop v7 audit and improvements

## Main findings

- The v6 homepage was accurate but visually dense, with a very large headline and a long sequence of similarly weighted sections.
- Navigation exposed the empty What’s On page, weakening usefulness. The route is preserved but now `noindex` and removed from primary navigation until enough verified events exist.
- Signup proof was weak. Dated public figures now come from one config object: 260 subscribers and 5 issues.
- Advertising packages needed clearer deliverables, dated evidence, capacity limits and a shorter enquiry form.
- Business submissions needed stronger grouping and a clearer distinction between editorial review and paid visibility.
- Mobile cards and footer structure needed stronger visual rhythm and larger, clearer actions.

## v7 outcome

- Balanced premium and family-friendly visual system.
- Stronger homepage proof, newsletter preview, clearer benefits and two signup opportunities.
- Dated advertiser evidence from Issue 5 main send plus the resend to non-openers, calculated against the original 256-recipient audience and not presented as an average.
- Clear £35 / £79 / £129 pilot packages with restrained sponsorship capacity.
- Simplified advertiser enquiry and structured local-information submission.
- Preserved logo, MailerLite forms, QR attribution, Cloudflare Functions, D1 schema and analytics hooks.
