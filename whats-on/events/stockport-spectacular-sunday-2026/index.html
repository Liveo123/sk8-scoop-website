Stockport's Spectacular Sunday: free street theatre and a giant snow lion

Stockport town centre is turning into a street-performance festival on Sunday 15 November, with more than 200 performers and over 50 free shows.

What you need to know

  • When: Sunday 15 November 2026, noon to 5pm.
  • Where: Merseyway, the Underbanks, Market Place, Redrock and nearby town-centre spaces.
  • Cost: Free to attend; food, shopping and travel are separate.
  • Finale: drummers' parade and Christmas lights switch-on at 4.45pm.

What will you see?

Expect a giant Snow Lion, circus and street theatre, live music, stiltwalkers and the Miniscule of Sound, a tiny nightclub with an unusually grand ambition.

Before you go: This is a walking event across several town-centre locations. Performance times or locations may change, especially in poor weather. Check the organiser's programme on the day.
