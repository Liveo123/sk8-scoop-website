Saturday 14 November · Tatton Park, Knutsford

Yuletide at Tatton Park

An illuminated outdoor folklore trail with live characters, music, puppetry and theatrical moments. Timed entry runs 3–7.30pm and the organiser suggests allowing about 90 minutes for the approximately 1km route. Source Issue 21 v25 listed adult £19.50 and child £11.50 tickets for 14 November, subject to recheck; consider the total family cost before booking.

A two-day festival of coffee tastings, talks, competitions and music. Public entry is on Saturday and Sunday, not the Friday trade day. Session ticket prices vary; organiser guidance listed £10 daily parking and free under-12 admission with the appropriate child ticket and paying adult. Good for serious coffee fans, less useful if you only want one cappuccino.
