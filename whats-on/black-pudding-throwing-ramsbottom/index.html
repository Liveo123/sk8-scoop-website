Why it made the Scoop

Because normal Sunday plans are overrated

The World Black Pudding Throwing Championships are one of those events that sound invented until you see the rules. Competitors stand on the oche and throw black puddings underarm at Yorkshire puddings perched on a 20-foot scaffold.

Each entrant gets three throws. The aim is simply to knock down as many Yorkshire puddings as possible. Watching is free, while anyone who fancies having a go can enter for a small fee on the day.

Why it matters: distinctive, cheap and memorable. It is close enough to SK8 to work as a Sunday excursion without turning into a full expedition.

What to expect

Expect a crowd, plenty of cheering, stalls and children’s rides, with the 2026 event also raising money for Bury Hospice and Ramsbottom Pantry.

Before you go: the event runs outdoors from 11am to 5pm. Check the organiser before travelling for any last-minute changes.
