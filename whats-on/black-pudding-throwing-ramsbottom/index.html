Why it made the Scoop

Because normal Sunday plans are overrated

The World Black Pudding Throwing Championships are one of those events that sound invented until you see the rules. Competitors stand below a scaffold and throw black puddings underarm at a stack of Yorkshire puddings set around 20 feet above the ground.

The winner is the person who knocks down the most targets with the permitted throws. Spectators can simply turn up and watch, while people who fancy entering pay a small fee.

Why it matters: NUE should surface things people would genuinely tell somebody else about. This is distinctive, cheap to watch and close enough to SK8 to work as a Sunday excursion rather than a full expedition.

What to expect

Expect a crowd, a lot of cheering and a contest that is competitive without taking itself too seriously. It works particularly well for readers who want a day out that feels different from another market, café or standard family attraction.

Before you go: the event is outdoors and popular. Check the organiser before travelling for the latest timings, competition details and any weather-related changes.
