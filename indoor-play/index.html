How we choose places

Our intended 100-point framework: play experience 30; value 20; practical family experience 15; parent experience 10; accessibility/inclusivity 10; review consistency 10; distinctiveness 5. We won't publish numeric scores until we have sufficient evidence across the criteria. Confidence is separate from quality; a sparse review record is not proof of a poor venue.

Moderate means useful desk research with unobserved details; limited means a material uncertainty. Our picks are based on the published features and costs, not payment or a safety inspection. Ask each venue about access needs and supervision.

Editorial independence: qualifying venues are listed free; nobody can buy a better score or pick. Advertising is separate and labelled. No gifted visit is claimed in this guide.
