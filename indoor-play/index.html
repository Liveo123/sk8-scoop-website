How we compare places

Our information coverage score is a weighted checklist of documented facts, not a review rating or inspected quality. Age and activity details 20; price detail 20 (full standard example 20, conditional published details 15, conflicting or partial details 10, child-only conflicting information 5, unknown 0); visit planning 15 (5 each for duration, booking and parking); parent facilities 10 (5 for documented refreshments, 5 reserved for independently checked viewing/comfort); accessibility information 10; comparable independent review evidence 10; distinctive documented features 5; 10 reserved for independently assessed play experience. Unknown evidence scores zero on coverage only, never on venue quality. No overall quality league table until fair evidence exists.

Scores help show which profiles have better documented information. For decisions, compare the published family prices, age fit, activities and sensory-session availability above. No paid rankings or safety inspections.

Editorial independence: qualifying venues are listed free; nobody can buy a better score or pick. Advertising is separate and labelled. No gifted visit is claimed in this guide.
